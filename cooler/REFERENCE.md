# Measured references and original design

The **Rill C48** is original geometry and fictional branding. All reference
images are inspection aids in ignored `refs/`; [SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md)
records their URLs and copyright. No model or photo-derived texture is used.

The [RTIC 45 QT manufacturer's dimension drawing](https://rticoutdoors.com/45-QT-Hard-Sided-Cooler)
shows an exterior 26.35 × 18.19 × 15.75 in (669.29 × 462.026 × 400.05 mm),
and cavity 18.90 × 11.42 × 10.98 in. It establishes the scale class, thick
wall, recessed carry area, gasket and rubber T-latch construction. The current
catalog hero may be a product visualization, so the separate outdoor photograph
is used for photographic critique. No real logo, rope layout or molded pattern
is reproduced.

[Pelican's 45QW specifications](https://www.pelican.com/us/en/product/coolers/45qw)
independently establish polyethylene shells and 50.8 mm polyurethane insulation
as a normal construction scale. Its wheeled configuration is not adopted.

Our authored lid is 673.1 × 419.1 mm and 419.1 mm from ground to crown. External
hinge/latch hardware adds depth. The body tapers from 658 × 404 mm at the
shoulder to 612 × 360 mm at the lower wall. The interior tapers from 555 ×
305 mm to 520 × 270 mm and is about 295 mm deep. These are design dimensions,
not a claim of exact catalog equivalence. The checker integrates cross-sectional
areas from actual inner mesh vertices to validate a 45–50 US-quart capacity;
a model name is not used as a capacity measurement. Radii, recessed grips,
hinges, gasket section and drain construction are independently authored.

Default wear describes ordinary suburban handling: sliding abrasion/dirt near
the floor, sun-softened upward plastic, polished grip/latch touch zones and
water deposits below the drain and in protected rim corners. No uniform dirt
noise substitutes for these physical masks.

For default used-object weather calibration, [a seller's actual used 45-quart
cooler](https://www.ebay.com/itm/404001940444) establishes full-frame abrasion
and grime placement; [Ryan McLean's own used cooler](https://huntingwaterfalls.com/how-to-remove-fix-scratches-from-a-yeti-cooler/)
establishes stress-whitened corner scratches and dark material caught in them.
Neither gives a measured service age. The much heavier damage in the seller
photo is not reproduced literally; the C48 targets ordinary suburban use.
A six-corner approximate camera fit gives 11.10 px RMS over a 635 px crop,
estimated 62.3 mm lens. This is a used-object comparison, distinct from the
earlier manufacturer outdoor photo whose age is unknown.
