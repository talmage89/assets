[Legacy APS](https://polara.com/inavigator), Polara, copyright retained:
manufacturer comparator for a cast button with raised directional arrow and
separate station/sign. [Current system manual](https://polara.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Polara-iNS-iDS-2Wire-System-Manual.pdf)
is a later construction comparator only; no Bluetooth/touchless controls enter
this 1995–2015 asset. The Cress case and speaker arrangement are original.

[332L cabinet sheet](https://www.econolite.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/SafeTran_cabinet-332L-datasheet_2021.pdf),
Econolite, copyright retained, 2021: later comparator for folded 0.125 in
aluminum, rain lip and service door. Its 67 × 24 × 30 in envelope is not the
Morrow Controls design's dimensions; our original cabinet is explicitly sized
separately in REFERENCE. No real certification marks are reproduced.

Derived neutral-balanced, cropped, gridded and rasterized reading aids retain
their original source's author and license and stay ignored in refs/. Photo
pairs crop the actual render and photo by the same pinhole rectangle; calibration
on the full image is retained separately. Comparison views omit neighborhood
context so it cannot conceal the fitted hardware. No photo contributes texels.
