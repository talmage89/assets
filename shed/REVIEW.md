# Review log / Hearthfield 810

## Pass 1 / source construction and used-photo comparison

Inspected `renders/eevee-review/{hero_closed,hero_open,interior,casting}.png`, the same four Cycles views in `renders/review-1/`, and `renders/source-photo-1/match_used-pair.jpg` / `renders/photo-1/match_used-pair.jpg`. Compared with the actual downloaded customer photo and Advanced Power Cleaning photo 11, not remembered shed imagery.

Tells: pale and smooth roof surface, overly long parallel lower-wall streaks, incorrect vertical grain on horizontal white trim, too-wide T1-11 groove spacing, and a hinge axis too close to the stile. The first sweep caught about 3 mm of collision. The first camera fit mixed a different doorway width into facade matching and supplied crop-relative pixels where the tool expects original-image pixels; it was unsuitable for proportion judgments.

Corrections: darkened asphalt with a second coarse mineral field that can survive the atlas; kept sparse real curled lips and shaded-slope staining; changed damp accumulation to a shorter, spatially broken field; aligned trim grain by timber direction; corrected grooves to 203.2 mm centers. Moved the hinge pin forward, split fixed bearings from the rotating sleeve, and added bent mounts connecting the ironwork to its supports. Added window cripples. Refit the used photograph from facade corners and ridge only: using original-image pixel coordinates: estimated 18.0 mm, RMS 16.4 px at the original crop size. The used shed is a different design and its eight-foot class is an estimate; this is a weathering/framing comparison, not a claim of exact identity.

The game asset remains under 10,000 triangles. Interior studs, plates, truss ties, rafters, plywood gussets, roof underside and deck are visible through the doors. The interior document view receives +1.25 EV to make the construction inspectable.

## Pass 2 / exported asset

Inspected all ten 1440 px Cycles renders in `renders/final/`, `renders/final-eevee/hero_closed.png`, the complete `renders/final/contact.jpg`, and `renders/delivery-review-glb/match_used-pair.jpg`. The export retains shingle laps and lifted tips, T1-11 grooves, visible lower-wall mildew/splash and the working framed interior. Door opening views show supported back rails and braces; the human stands beside the shed at actual 1.8 m scale. Measured geometry is 9,220 triangles, two materials and three shared 2048² maps, GLB 10,926,412 bytes. Both 23-sample hinge sweeps report zero penetration; jack/header support separation is below 0.24 μm, and the skid/joist and rafter/sheathing contacts intersect. Independent vertex measurements caught and corrected the nominal-versus-clear doorway dimension in the README.

`make -C shed` and `make -C shed check` passed. The final 4096 bake reports zero missed interior samples and zero black BaseColor interior samples; imported vertices/pivots agree exactly with the expected source. These establish serialization separately from the physical clearance and support checks. The shared kit export-policy fixture passes after the optional human-offset addition.

Weakest: the full interior and exterior share one unique 2048 atlas. Fine plywood fiber, chipped-paint edges and asphalt mineral detail soften in close-ups; the roof remains more regular than the reference. The threshold reads as exposed rubbed wood rather than deeply blackened old timber. These are acknowledged limits of this few-season, budgeted game asset; no claim of photographic indistinguishability is made. The used reference has different siding, door width and uncertain absolute dimensions, so the match is a wear/framing comparison, not metrology for that individual shed.

## Phase 2 / caused wear and three-tab finishing

Inspected the used reference photos again, then the exported `renders/wear-review-glb/match_used-pair.jpg` and both exported views in `renders/wear-check-glb/`. The uniform lower-wall wash is gone: splash is confined to the lowest siding/skirt, with separate shaded groove/corner/under-trim deposits, sill runoff, sunny chalking and pull-area handling. Each door’s material now uses its rest-pivot offset, preventing the lower local Z origin from painting a false wash up the leaf. Skids/rim use the kit’s `weathered_wood()` in separate timber grain frames plus a grade-aligned weather frame. Framing, interior, doors and pivots are preserved.

The first roof export still looked smooth, despite the corrected twelve-course layout, half-tab offsets, 6 mm cutouts and strip-based color. Rejected that smooth result. Strengthened original mineral cluster color/relief so it survives the shared atlas, widened the strip granule-blend range, and made shaded moss a soft, irregular moisture field. Accepted the revised roof/hero in `renders/wear-check-2-glb/` and final exported photo pair `renders/wear-review-2-glb/match_used-pair.jpg`. Lifted tips remain geometry. Nominal 127 mm exposure is cited in `REFERENCE.md`; twelve fitted courses measure 127.8 mm along this roof’s slope. The roof alone adds 480 triangles, staying below the existing 10,000 budget.

The photo’s older bare plywood, different doorway and horizontal gable siding remain different from our green painted design. The pair judges wear location and exposed timber aging, not exact paint color; the existing 18 mm lens estimate and 16.4 px RMS are unchanged. The original customer photo also informs the roof’s rectangular asphalt-tab proportions. Finest individual granules merge into visible aggregate clusters at 123 px/m; the exported close-up is still weaker than a dedicated roof trim/tile atlas. The plywood threshold remains warmer than the gray-brown treated rim/skids, as it is exposed foot-rubbed plywood, not treated framing.
