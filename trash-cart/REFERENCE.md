# Wheeled trash cart reference and measurements

The controlling envelope is Toter's 96-gallon EVRII 79296 manufacturer's
[specification table](https://www.toter.com/product/two-wheel-carts/):
**35½ in deep × 29¾ in wide × 43¼ in tall**, or **901.70 × 755.65 × 1098.55 mm**.
The [manufacturer brochure](https://www.toter.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/EVRII_Brochure.pdf)
p5 supplies **10 in / 254 mm wheels**; p4 supplies a **5/8 in / 15.875 mm axle**.
The brochure's 43½-inch height differs by 6.35 mm; do not mix that revision into
this envelope. 96 US gallons is nominally 363.40 L, a product capacity class,
not a promise of this original interior's measured usable capacity.

The authored cart is fictional **City of Alder Creek / AC 096-047281**.
The product photographs establish construction, not a copied mesh/profile:
front lower lifting channel and rotating steel bar, broad upper saddle,
side gripper shoulders, wheel clearances, integral rear grip, pin hinges,
reinforced rolled rim and crowned lid. Pixel anchors are review camera
measurements; no photographic outline is traced or projected into geometry.

The brief specifies rotomolded HDPE. This fictional cart uses that material;
the cited Toter brochure p7 calls its own resin MDPE. No claim is made that
this is a manufacturing-exact reproduction or a certified lifter interface.
Original hot-stamp graphics are rasterized from open-license DejaVu Sans.
The standard recycling symbol uses compact molded geometry generated from
the same installed font. Sun exposure, dragging, truck grips, touch and
water runoff define weather masks. Reference credits and URLs are in
[refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md).
