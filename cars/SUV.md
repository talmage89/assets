# Alder X220 — form round 2

Original compact crossover, generated entirely by scripts in Blender 5.2.
This checkpoint sculpts the ends, hood, mirrors and side of the accepted package.
It stops before optics, handles, interior, underbody, finishes, UVs, baking and
SUV export. The [family index](README.md), [reference comparisons](REFERENCE.md),
[reference credits](refs/SOURCES.md) and [critique history](REVIEW.md) accompany it.

## Face, rear and plan

The one-sheet parent now has a broad central plan, swept corners, a raised hood
center with two draw lines, a ridge above each tapered corner pocket, a convex
gate, a stamped plate surround and a broad rear bumper crown. The hexagonal
folded grille lip and curved blades are Alder's original treatment. Painted
corners descend around the lower intake. Separate solid cladding remains.

The two rescue drawings supply sparse plan picks; they are not engineering CAD.
[The Toyota sheet](https://global.toyota/pages/global_toyota/your-vehicle/RAV4_50_1.pdf)
and [Honda RW sheet](https://w3.techinfo.honda-eu.com/sites/default/files/2023-11/cr-v_rw-_en.pdf)
are independently scaled by the cited envelopes. Honda's drawing precedes the
facelift. Allow approximately ±20 mm plan and ±30 mm photographic height
uncertainty, plus trim/projection differences. The rear plan follows Honda;
Toyota's corner is about 41 mm farther back. Source rays sample the most outward
actual hit over 250–1345 mm height at 5 mm intervals. The XY radii use three
sampled envelope points; neither end is claimed constant-radius.

The reference rear lower-lip figures exclude the additional black upper
bumper/gate band. Counting that band gives 407 mm Toyota and 276 mm Honda;
calling those entire areas a 230 mm lip would be misleading. The source lower
heights come from physical plane cuts, not vertex bands or design inputs.

The front lamps grow toward the outer corner, taper into the fender and share
the hood's upper edge. Polar surface charts keep their upper edges off the hood
and remove the former torn cuts. Each taillamp is one wrapped guide split at
the 4.5 mm gate reveal. Its closed cassette is 42 mm deep. All 42 sampled
opening/optical clearance rays find clear apertures. The plate has a supported
overhead lamp. Rear reflector and exhaust pockets are cut, with a separate
central skid finish; final optical and exhaust detail remains deferred.

The gate uses a stamped transverse crease and unmarked badge land. A separate
narrow garnish trial was removed because it read as a tube. The closed spoiler
has a molded underside, dropping end caps and an integrated brake-lamp blank.
Its 23 measured root samples overlap the roof by 1.5–4.1 mm: it is seated, with
a real overhang behind the root. Rails stand 39–47 mm above the local roof on
two broad molded feet each; their absolute maximum remains 1700 mm.

## Mirrors and side sculpture

Each mirror is **260 mm outboard × 160 mm high × 130 mm deep**, with a compound
rounded nose and a planar rear glass face. Housing centers are at
**X ±928.5, Y −649, Z 1215 mm**, bottoms at **1135 mm**. The small sails begin
at the upper front door, Y −890 mm, behind the A-foot; each assembly belongs to
its front door. The 4 mm glass thickness is distinguished from its flat face.
The side camera shows no mirror bulge on the hood/cowl profile. Complete moving
mirror construction is still outside this form stage.

50 mm chord has a 2.05–2.07 mm hollow. Fender swells, measured at Z 900 mm
against the middle door, are **32.4 mm front / 29.3 mm rear**. Adjacent fender
crowns are 7.3–10.0 mm above the actual hood edge. Hood center-to-X600 mm drop

The raking sun now runs forward along the body, avoiding the old long mirror
shadow across the doors. The shoulder is a continuous narrow highlight, the
lower door draws a rising light/dark break, and the rear arch swell is distinct.
The front swell is subtler in this particular view; the three-quarter and band
views remain necessary. Actual side-pane central chords remain 19.0–19.4°.

## Construction and allocation

A single 1900-quad sheet is refined twice and fitted at its **control vertices**,
then Catmull–Clark evaluated. Its 30,400 control quads remain connected and
four-valent internally. No evaluated parent vertex is fitted afterward. Game
samples, projected panel cuts and separate 0.9 mm steel / 3 mm fascia gauges
follow. End apertures use local constrained triangulation in a polar chart;
new boundary points are sampled from the fair parent. Untouched triangles
retain their positions. Main glass is 4.8 mm, and actual sampled door gaps
remain 4.500 mm. Hidden gauge reduction is rejected if its probes emerge
through the exposed sheet. The inherited parent normals are restored after cuts.

**59,642 game triangles; 141 mesh objects; five diagnostic materials; zero image
textures.** No SUV GLB exists at this gate. The finishing target is at most
65,000 triangles and one 2048 BaseColor / Normal / ORM set, approximately
16 MiB compressed with mipmaps. Object consolidation and final materials remain
part of delivery; these source object counts are not shipping draw calls.

## Rebuild, evidence and limits

```sh
make -C cars BODY=suv WIDTH=1600 SAMPLES=40
make -C cars BODY=suv check
make -C cars BODY=suv check-repeat
```

`BODY=pickup` remains the default. The SUV targets fetch only ignored reference
images/PDFs, build both densities, measure actual game surfaces, audit both
sources, check parent/game curvature, verify the same six cameras, render through
the shared GPU lock and compose the evidence. No kit code changed. The only
shared code change is an optional tire-segment setting in `wheels.py`, with
identical existing defaults; the SUV uses 48 circumferential samples.

Current evidence is `renders/suv-round2/`: `contact.jpg`, all 24 source renders,
four calibrated photo pairs in clay **and** stripes, perspective/orthographic
overlays, side sections, curvature plots, reference measurement annotations,
plan measurements and two round-1/round-2 comparison sheets. All source cameras
are unchanged. The old evidence remains in `renders/suv-form/`. No checkpoint
image or rebuildable asset is committed.

The four chassis-fit RMS values remain 7.19 / 8.91 / 8.40 / 9.99 native pixels;
side fits hold the original estimated 85 mm lens. Projection checks validate
camera implementation, not body correspondence. Sparse outline holdouts have
approximately ±15.5 mm pick uncertainty before camera/trim uncertainty. Fixed-axis
and nearest-silhouette residuals are both retained; a steep D-pillar amplifies
the former. No body outline is fitted or used to generate geometry.

The lower front and rear improvements are within approximately 40 mm of both
makes. The spoiler follows Honda; Toyota's 44 mm miss is disclosed. This is
**not** a whole-outline 40 mm result: the accepted Honda D-pillar mismatch
remains approximately 101 mm nearest / 211 mm fixed-column height. The raw
orthographic overlays remain in the evidence and are not substituted for the
specified perspective comparison.

The complete make recipe, `make check` and `make check-repeat` pass. Game/high
source audits have zero errors/warnings. Both eleven-section curvature reports
pass the named feature zones while retaining all raw reversals. Their expanded
rear zones describe the new bumper crown/waist and lower return; the bands
still reveal localized ripple, so passing is not a global fairness claim.
The independent repeat matches **154 objects / 141 meshes** exactly, including
geometry, corner normals, transforms, parents and material names.

The game source is **1,464,207 bytes / 59,642 triangles**; high source
**2,814,459 bytes / 124,584 triangles**. Both are ignored rebuild artifacts.
All 24 renders carry source SHA-256 `dc372cca0ed7a67e5dda728a241d07ae2c6a5d1c094cdcea952f8ca22906b020`.
Hero foreground luminance p99 is **230 / 218**, with zero clipped channels.
The relit raking view has median **53**, p99 **185**. Neither paint albedo nor
final texture/delivery quality is certified by these clay values.

A fresh pickup source build and re-export of its unchanged finalized/maps
match **454 source meshes / 150 GLB accessors**, embedded textures and graph.
Both GLBs are byte-identical at **11,530,740 bytes**, SHA-256
`ec70cde87fbe50fb814cc14c900ee324e254711b5bfa5d3735aef166b146de5b`.
Proof is `build/suv/pickup-round2-preservation.json`; this checks the shared
wheel-default change, not a new pickup texture bake.

Logs are under the clone's ignored `build/`: `suv-round2-final-model.log`,
`suv-round2-final-check.log`, `suv-round2-final-render.log`,
`suv-round2-final-review.log`, `suv-round2-repeat.log` and
`suv-pickup-round2*.log`. Mechanical sweeps, UV/texel density, baking,
GLB/re-import and final shipping budgets remain explicitly deferred.

The front reads more contemporary through its full-height lamps, close grille
junction, hood draws and descending painted corners. The rear is still weaker:
the broad plate shoulder is soft, and grazing bands expose localized waviness
around the bumper turn and D-pillar transition. Mirror highlights remain less
fair than an injection-molded production housing. Dark optical blanks, regular
window corners and missing finishing parts still make the white clay read as a
prototype. These are disclosed form limitations, not excuses to declare it a
finished car. The lead should judge the new pairs before any detail stage.
