# Reference and dimensional decisions

This is the fictional **Bellford H-54**, an original three-way American
traffic-pattern dry-barrel hydrant, set directly into grade. References supply
measurements and manufacturing observations; no reference pixels or geometry
are imported, sampled or traced into the asset.

The controlling dimension sheet is Mueller's [FH-84 revision L](https://www.muellercompany.com/sites/muellercompany.com/files/uploads/media/fh-84_rev-l.pdf),
a 2019-style Super Centurion 350 drawing. The [250 parts poster](https://www.muellercompany.com/sites/muellercompany.com/files/uploads/media/super_centurion_250_parts_pstr_11966_web.pdf)
and product photo show the same basic three-way construction. These are related
examples of a common type, not a claim to reproduce a manufacturer model or its
pressure certification. Dimensions on FH-84 are explicitly reference dimensions.

The 8.18 in sheet callout aligns the hose extension with the hydrant axis.
Other uncalled sizes are estimates with their uncertainty, not dimensions
claimed from CAD. Cast profiles and rib shapes are original. The game mesh uses
24-sided cylindrical profiles, higher source tessellation, fused cast nozzle
throats, capped exterior caps, and an installed ground stub. The buried valve,
shoe and full underground riser are deliberately omitted from this above-grade
street prop. A capped outlet has no visible bore, seal or thread interior.

FH-84 lists cast iron for the barrel, bonnet, caps and traffic flange; bronze
for nozzles/operating hardware, and electrogalvanized steel for the cap chains.
The separate flange seam, cap reveals, dome ribs, wrench flats and chains follow
that assembly logic. The [installed rear photograph by Daderot](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fire_hydrant,_Mueller_5.25_2000,_Albertville_AL_-_Arlington,_MA_-_DSC00425.jpg)
(CC0) supplies the red/white repaint appearance, casting scale, chalking, chips
on operating hardware, and grime at the base. The photo depicts an older
casting; exact lettering, lower ribs and bonnet details intentionally differ.
The fictional raised BELLFORD / WATER WORKS / H-54 / 2021 marks replace all
manufacturer names, logos and approval stamps. White is an appearance choice;
no jurisdiction-specific flow classification is implied.

`WEAR=.55` represents a maintained, repainted suburban hydrant. UV chalking
follows exposure, cap/flange edge chips reveal iron and rust, bolt tracks run
with gravity, and road/soil splash darkens the lower 190 mm. Noise only breaks
these causal masks or describes the sand-cast surface. `WEAR=0` suppresses use
wear while retaining casting microstructure.
