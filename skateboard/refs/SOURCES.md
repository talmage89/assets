## Consulted measurement pages (not downloaded)

- [Skate One manufacturing and shape chart](https://www.skateone.com/manufacturing), Skate One, copyright retained: maple deck and published envelope. Current chart cross-checks the period catalog; no shape data imported.
- [Action Sports Technology skateboard molds](https://www.actionsportstech.com/index.php/machinery/skateboard-molds), manufacturer, copyright retained: Version 2 street mold 18° nose/tail and .30 in concave at 8 in; Version 1 19.5°/19° and .35 in. Our original mold uses these as a dimensional class, not a recovered mold.
- [Independent truck chart](https://independenttrucks.com/trucks), manufacturer, copyright retained: standard 55 mm axle height and size matching; current catalog is only an engineering cross-check, not evidence all sizes existed throughout 1995–2015.
- [NSK 608 boundary dimensions](https://www.nsk.com/eu-en/engineering/calculation/calculate/index/sku/608-ESM_MD/), NSK, copyright retained: bore 8, outside 22, width 7 mm.
- [SKF ISO designations](https://cdn.skfmediahub.skf.com/api/public/094d51a87837ab50/pdf_preview_medium/SKF_verso_A3_FR_pdf_preview_medium.pdf), SKF, copyright retained: ISO designation 608 = 8 × 22 × 7. 608 is a designation, not an ISO standard number. Boundary-dimension standard is ISO 15.
