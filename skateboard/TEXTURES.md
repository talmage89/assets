# Texture and age plan — not built at this form checkpoint

One 4096 source bake, one 2048 shipped BaseColor / OpenGL +Y Normal / ORM set.
One delivered material across the board's rigid groups. At 8 bits per compressed
texel per map, with full mip chains: 2048² × 3 × 4/3 = 16,777,216 bytes,
**16 MiB**. The proposed two-set option would cost 20 MiB; it is not needed.
No final maps or atlas are claimed by this checkpoint's quick material GLB.

Reserve roughly 65% of the atlas for upper/lower deck faces and rails, 20% for
castings and hardware, 15% for wheels/bearing faces. This is a packing plan,
not a measured texel-density result. Actual 4096 UV priority and density must
be measured at the next gate; reconsider this split if the 608 shields lose
readability in the carried view. Repeated wheel graphics share a generated
sheet, while each worn contact band can use a distinct causal mask.

Fictional maker proposal: **Ditchlight** decks, **Breakwater** trucks,
**Lowbank** wheels. No graphics have been made yet. Three seeded original
bottom concepts: a night drainage map built from authored lines and lettering;
a moth assembled from original geometric ink shapes; a fictional municipal
broadcast test card. These are drawings to be generated by scripts, never
photographs or third-party art. One small fictional shop sticker on the front
foot's inboard grip margin; its scale must preserve the grip's black reading.
Use installed open-license fonts through `kit/fonts.py`.

The age field is registered (`new`, `season`, `beat`) but has no visual effect
yet. This keeps the checkpoint about the original construction. The next stage
must implement all three, including the geometric damage where necessary:

- New: maple end grain and seven veneer edges, filed silicon-carbide grip edge,
  rail score line, cast grain, turned bearing rings and molded urethane.
- Season: directional boardslide abrasion between trucks and lipslide tracks
  near the mounting region, graphic/ink worn through to maple; tail-ground
  abrasion, exposed/chipped ply ends, localized nose impacts; front-foot dirt
  and grip polishing at stance/tail; bright ground hanger crowns scored across
  the axle; coned/stained wheel treads and scuffed bolt heads.
- Beat: thinner razor tail, discrete chipped/delaminating ply tips, an original
  shoe-goo repair patch, torn nose grip. Keep these geometric changes local;
  never apply a whole-board noise wash. Watch budget on the damaged seed.

Wear must be calibrated against the used 2010 board in matched sun, measured
in the exported GLB, then checked at full hero frame. The period mini photograph
has green wheels and blue paint; those colors are not our original palette.
The red underside is only a quick diagnostic color, not approved look-dev.
