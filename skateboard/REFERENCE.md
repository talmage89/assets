# Form sources and authored choices

The default is an original 8 inch popsicle street board, compatible with American
1995–2015 construction. The [Spring 2014 Skate One catalog](https://www.skateone.com/assets/pdf/2014-spting-skate-one-catalog.pdf)
shape table (112, 127, 188 and 170) is the period dimensional baseline. Default
shape-class 127: 8 × 32.125 in, 14.375 in inner-row wheelbase, 6.875 in nose,
6.625 in tail. With two 2.125 inch mounting patterns these sum to 32.125 in.
Model uses plan distances; pressed-surface tape measurements can differ.
No manufacturer's perimeter or mold is traced or imported.

[Action Sports Technology's Version 2 mold](https://www.actionsportstech.com/index.php/machinery/skateboard-molds)
specifies 18° kicks and .30 in concave at 8 in width. These establish our mold
class: 7.62 mm rail rise, 43 mm eased kick transition, rounded asymmetric nose
and tail. Seven 1 mm veneers, edge roundover, 0.55 mm grip thickness and the
local flattening of truck seats are construction estimates, not published gauges.
Seven veneers have alternating long/cross grain; fine grain and glue lines wait
for the normal map. One continuous closed deck has seven exposed rail bands;
there are no hidden stacked internal faces.

[Open Source Skateboards' finishing guide](https://v1.opensourceboards.com/OSS-Curriculum.pdf)
publishes the 1.625 × 2.125 in four-hole pattern: 41.275 × 53.975 mm centers.
Eight 5 mm holes are drilled through the deck and grip; steel countersunk
10-32 bolts sit in conical seats. 7/8 inch hardware without risers; 1 inch with
1/8 inch risers. Clearance hole and head dimensions are authored hardware estimates.

[Independent's specification](https://independenttrucks.com/trucks) supplies
55 mm standard mounting-face-to-axle height. Matched axle width equals deck
width; default 203.2 mm tip to tip, cast hanger 137 mm between axle exits.
The hanger, pivot stem, open kingpin yoke and baseplate pedestal are original
sculpted unions, not a silhouette extrusion. Plate envelope 58 × 80 mm,
kingpin 9.525 mm diameter and 20° inclination, 12.7/10.2 mm bushings, and
50° virtual steering axis are measured/photo-informed construction estimates.
There is no claim to recovered proprietary truck geometry. The virtual axis
joins the pivot-cup center to the yoke/bushing center; it is NOT simply the
kingpin shaft. Travel is a conservative ±12° assembly rotation, to be checked
on actual wheel/deck surfaces. Bushing flex is not simulated.

The period catalog lists a 54 × 32 mm V1 street wheel. Our original rounded
profile has a 15 mm nominal cylindrical riding band; the shoulder and recess
radii are photo estimates. Hard urethane does not have a pneumatic tire's flat
contact patch. A geometric line contact is retained at rest.
[NSK's 608 table](https://www.nsk.com/eu-en/engineering/calculation/calculate/index/sku/608-ESM_MD/)
gives 8 × 22 × 7 mm. 608 is the ISO bearing *designation*, not a standard named
“ISO 608”: boundary dimensions follow ISO 15. Two shielded bearings, 10 mm
spacer, 0.5 mm speed rings, 5/16 inch axle nuts and their nylon inserts are
modeled. Internal balls/cages and helical threads will not consume game triangles.

Seed 0 is the default; 1/2/3 cover 7.75/8.25/8.5 inch deck classes, 51/53/56 mm
wheels, matching truck widths and a 3.175 mm riser on seed 3. These additional
molds are original envelopes in the requested ranges. New/season/beat are
registered as surface states; causal age, graphics and damage are deferred at
this explicitly form-only checkpoint.
