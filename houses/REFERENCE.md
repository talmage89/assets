# Builder colonial: reference and authored dimensions

This is an original 1990–2010 American builder-colonial grammar, not a copy of
one photographed house or purchased plan. References supply construction
vocabulary, dimensional bounds and proportion checks. Nothing is traced,
projected, imported as geometry or sampled into an asset texture.

## Evidence and limits

The [Maryland SHA 2019 suburbanization context addendum](https://www.roads.maryland.gov/OPPEN/Suburbanization%20Context%20Addendum_Final-2019.pdf),
Appendix F, identifies modern-colonial two-story massing with an attached
garage set forward and a shallow entrance porch. It supports the type, not
this design's dimensions or a claim about the photographed houses' dates.

The `colonial-1991-oh` reference, from northeast Ohio,
is listed as built in **1991**. Its photograph shows a two-story principal
block, six-over-six grilles, paired shutters, siding, side-gable roof and
front-facing double garage. Our first proof has a hip main roof and a room
over the garage; the comparison crop judges the principal facade. The photo
has no survey or reliable focal metadata.

The `colonial-rog-ri` reference, from Rhode Island,
shows the room-over-garage variation, front gable, corner posts, window trim,
and deeper sill/casing shadows. It also has two single doors, a projecting
entrance bay and a higher stepped foundation. Those are **not** features or
measurements of our proof house. The image's 2017 watermark does not establish construction date.

The 4 ft overlap is a **union**, not two complete houses placed through each
other. Intersecting wall skins are removed below the adjacent roof; windows
are rejected in covered wall areas; the garage owns the overlapping first
floor area and the lower room above. Rooms in the main block stop at that
interface. No hidden floor slabs, framing or interiors consume the mesh budget.

Roof planes are clipped at their actual intersections. Hip variants need a
narrow, tapered metal cricket where the garage would otherwise create a dead
valley. That saddle is geometry with falling side valleys. Gable variants
have diagonal draining valleys and need no saddle.

## Seed grammar and limits

Default ranges: principal width 38/40/42 ft, depth 26/28/29 ft; garage width
and depth 22/23/24 ft; projection 4/5/6 ft; main roof hip or gable at
6/7/8:12; garage left/right; upper-front rhythm four/five bays; gabled/hipped
portico; front veneer on/off; six restrained color palettes. Windows obey
room roles rather than independent random positions. Four lower-front bays
reserve entry space; the garage connection removes occluded ones.

`generate(seed, overrides)` accepts the same small nested plan as the JSON
CLI input. Two rectangular two-story blocks and a portico are the supported
grammar. This run deliberately keeps the room-over-garage variant. One-story
garages, arbitrary polygons, dormers and other house types are future grammar
extensions, not unvalidated random fallbacks.

Reference downloads, authors and rights: [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md).

## Construction revision and source look checkpoint

The `colonial-2003-nh` listing reference
publishes 2003 construction, wood framing, vinyl siding, concrete foundation,
architectural shingles, an attached two-car garage, a gas fireplace and kitchen
sliders to a deck. Those are published facts about the reference property,
not dimensions of our asset. Its house is 23 years old in 2026. The summer
photograph's capture date is unknown; listing publication does not establish
the photo's exact age. Photo rights remain reserved and no pixels enter maps.

Picked original-image anchors for the added comparison are (61,501),
(600,500), (61,246), (600,256), (326,501), paired with main facade floor/plate
and entry-center points. The fitted front plane has 8.48 px RMS residual,
weaker than the original colonial-1991-oh 3.34 / colonial-rog-ri 4.12 px fits. Its lens reaches
the 100 mm crop-camera bound. Its gabled front and lower garage are different
builder choices, not errors to remove by changing our accepted hip/garage form.

Albedos are authored physical targets, not values inferred from compressed
listing photos. Warm-white vinyl plateau factors are .70/.69/.65 linear;
stored overlap shadows lower the average. The neutral charcoal shingle map
averages approximately .10 linear. Red-brown brick and poured concrete have
separate fields. The photo pairs compare perceived color/value while allowing
for different exposure, sun direction, landscaping and the authored palette.
