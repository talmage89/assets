Additional primary specifications, read online (no model/CAD download):

- [emu Ronda](https://emuamericas.com/residential/collections/ronda/products/ronda-e116), tube/expanded-steel construction and dimensions. Current sheet is a construction comparator, not evidence for an exact chair's 2008 sales date.
- [emu Nova](https://emuamericas.com/commercial/collections/nova/products/nova-28-sq-e858), 29 in dining-height / 27.5 in top comparator. Our 28 in round open-lattice top and pedestal are original choices, not this square product.
- [BUNN VP17 family catalog](https://resource.bunn.com/library/pdf/2019_us_catalog_hyperlinks_1-29-19_web.pdf), p20, VP17-1 17.30 × 8.40 × 18.80 in H/W/D. Later publication supports the conventional brewer envelope, not a claim of a specific 1997 SKU.
- [La Marzocco legacy technical archive](https://au.lamarzocco.com/support/), explicitly distinguishes legacy Linea Classic from Classic S released June 2022. No modern touch screen/pro-app fittings appear in our machine.

Local newspaper/archive research:

Period independent/regional cafés, pastry counters and small patios; no names, addresses, real menus or graphics copied.
- [Tom Fucoloro, Capitol Hill Seattle News, 18 August 2011](https://www.capitolhillseattle.com/2011/08/capitol-hill-sidewalks-get-bigger-new-rules-make-it-easier-to-have-outside-cafe-seating/). Period curbside café change, parked-car protection and 3 ft curb/car-door clearance. Governing dimensions are taken from the municipal material above, not certified from the article.
