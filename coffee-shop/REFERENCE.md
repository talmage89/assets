# Coffee shop / dimensional construction record

This is an original family, rather than a reconstruction of a particular
business. **Published comparators and authored choices are separated below.**
Photographs establish period, assembly and density; none supplies vertices,
trace outlines, decals, textures or lighting. Rights and direct retrieval URLs
are in [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md).

## Shops and period

The 2008 Commons photographs show an independent Main Street cafe/bakery in
an older shell, a regional end-cap coffee shop with a covered walk, and an
independent coffee shop in a converted service-station building. Coffea's
2008 side patio supplies the furniture/umbrella comparison. Cafe Rose
Nicaud supplies the counter, menu and pastry-case construction comparison.
The 2001 Houston Chronicle survey confirms the period's independent and
regional shops, small patios, espresso equipment and pastry service.
Neither real trade dress nor photographed people/cars enter the models.

## Photo framing and color discipline

`src/matches.py` uses `kit/photo_match.calibrate` and the kit's independent
Blender projection check. Named pixels are measured in a 1000 px study, with
explicit crops. The four front-glazing anchors at Lakeview are on the same
plane; an earlier candidate accidentally mixed its side glazing into that
plane and was rejected. The original building's large projecting roof still
differs from our pad. Lens limits and residuals remain visible in each pair.

Before comparison, neutral-balance assumptions are recorded: Annie's pale
painted door trim, Coffea's unpainted metal table edge, and Lakeview's pale
window/base trim. These are assumptions, not gray-card captures. White balance
uses linear-channel gains and preserves exposure. The dark steel is not dyed
blue to match shaded photographic pixels. Final checkpoint heroes retain a
fixed −1 exposure, a 26° procedural sun and desaturated procedural sky; there
is no image environment or exposure-based concrete calibration.

## Checks and their limits

`build/check.json` records imported triangle counts, individual measured
envelopes, actual surface intersections with the drawn route boxes, knee
space rays, canonical image identities and existing-delivery hashes.
`build/photo-calibration.json` records photographic residuals; the independent
projection test addresses camera implementation, not photographic accuracy.
`build/presentation-check.json` addresses current export/image provenance and
display tonal range. None of these proves final material realism, unique bake
UV quality, whole-site ADA/permit compliance, or continuous swept-door clearance.

## Look-dev construction additions

The converted pad now has a low standing-seam hip, taking its broad metal
roof/eave vocabulary from the June 2008 Lakeview Brew photograph in SOURCES.
The bank-branch conversion story is an original design choice; that photo is
not claimed to document a bank. The original roof is 3.70 m at the eave and
5.16 m at the short ridge, with 460 mm eaves, 406.4 mm seam pitch and 19 mm
locks. A cantilevered 1.30 × 2.90 m walk-up cover uses wall brackets and keeps
the accepted side route unobstructed. These are authored construction sizes,
not dimensions surveyed from the photograph.

Awning ribs retain the original spacing (approximately 880 mm). The cloth
now drops 95 mm between ribs, with corner draw-in and a loose 200–238 mm
valance; welded carriers remain beneath it. This is an original tensioned
canvas surface, compared with the hanging/scalloped fabric in the period
references, not a cloth simulation or a traced photographic surface.

Pastries are original hand-sized forms: croissant roughly 110 × 65 × 45 mm,
muffin about 68 mm diameter × 70 mm, scone about 80 mm across × 36 mm, cookie
76 mm diameter × 12 mm. Their size is an authored culinary estimate, checked
against the case shelves and 148 mm saucer, not a manufacturer specification.
No food scans or photographic texels are used.

The seeded contexts reuse this repository's dressed Downtown storefronts,
Commercial tenant bays, cars, house, lights, grates/fixtures and vegetation.
The Main Street road is 14 m wide with a curbside parking lane; strip/pad
stalls use a 2.74 m pitch and 5.4 m depth, with a nominal 6.096 m circulation
aisle. Site layouts are original bounded compositions. The geometric café
clearance proof still applies to the unchanged accepted seating routes; it
is not extended into a whole-site parking/permit certification.
