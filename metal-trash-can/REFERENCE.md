# 31-gallon galvanized steel garbage can

Sources are listed in [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md). Manufacturer catalog (2021), trash/utility cans, stock 1270K: 31 US gallons, 21 × 21 × 29.75 inches including lid. Model target: **533.40 mm lid diameter × 755.65 mm overall height**. The current product page also says 23 inches in its dimension subsection, whereas its table still says 21; we use the dated catalog, not a mixture of revisions. The current page's 18-inch bottom diameter gives a **457.20 mm lower chime**.

Observed construction: a tapered corrugated sheet-steel body, folded longitudinal seam, rolled mouth, elevated bottom panel enclosed by a lower chime, removable shallow pressed lid with concentric stiffening beads and a folded edge; attached carrying handles. This is an original classic design, without the manufacturer's patented lid geometry, labels, or trademarks.

Authored construction estimates, not published measurements: 0.65 mm wall, 0.8 mm lid, 24 vertical corrugations, 5 mm flute depth, 8 mm mouth curl, 16 mm raised floor, 6 mm wire side handles and 22 mm-wide bent lid bridge. Dents are restrained local impacts; white corrosion originates at wet hems and seam; rubbing polishes handle contact. Capacity is nominal, not a certified internal volume.
