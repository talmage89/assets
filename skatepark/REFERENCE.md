# Dimensions and construction basis

All geometry is original. Published dimensions inform a construction vocabulary;
these fictional parks do not reproduce a real designer's plan. Photos are
unsurveyed form/age comparators. Sources and rights are in [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md).

The skating slab is a terrain skin. Only exposed skins and recessed saw-cut
strips are retained around poured features; no flat floor spans the bowl.
Poured features represent reinforced concrete over prepared fill: internal
rebar and buried foundations are not exported. Smooth riding concrete and
the broom-finished pedestrian walk must use different finish responses in
look-dev, while sharing the new concrete manufacture tile.

The photo matches fit an original asset to selected construction landmarks,
not every detail of a photographed park. The bowl phases are inferred contour
correspondences; the comparison camera height is an original-scale fit, not
the photographer's measured eye height. Estimated focal lengths apply to
the cropped canvases. All residuals remain in the calibration report. The
Memorial fit includes an outside deck point to disambiguate the camera's
side of the coping. Its tile-bottom landmarks follow the actual circular
section, rather than an invented vertical fascia.
