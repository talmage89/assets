Additional primary specifications consulted (HTML, not downloaded):

- [Rhino technical construction](https://www.rhino-ramps.com/public/technical-info): enclosed ventilated galvanized sides, flush toe/transition plates, coping, protected seams, stainless screws. Construction comparator, not a copied product.
- [Techramps SM005](https://www.techramps.com/en/skatepark-designs,ii/example-objects,ibg/sample-skatepark-sm005,behfji): modular quarter 300 × 366 × 120cm. Used for the order of magnitude of a 1.2m high prefabricated quarter, not exact dimensions or geometry.
- [Skatelite resources](https://www.skatelite.com/resources): riding-sheet installation and screw-pattern documentation; phenolic riding-sheet construction. Fictional unbranded material in this asset.
- [Rockville equipment RFP 20-17](https://www.rockvillemd.gov/DocumentCenter/View/18431/RFP-20-17-Skate-Park-FINAL?bidId=): phenolic laminate, vented enclosed backs/sides, recessed weather-resistant fasteners, steel grind armor. Later specification documents the same construction vocabulary; not evidence of the fictional 1999 installation date.
- [Federal Stone 12in SBN](https://www.federalstone.com/product-page/12-sbn): nominal 12in top, 2.5in bullnose, 24in length; [skate application](https://www.federalstone.com/fedstoneskate). Authored curved block segmentation differs from straight catalog stock.
- [US Access Board, outdoor developed areas](https://www.access-board.gov/guidelines-and-standards/recreation-facilities/outdoor-developed-areas/): pedestrian clear spaces and park amenities. Scope applies to federal outdoor facilities; used as a dimensional comparator, not a municipal compliance claim.
- [US Access Board ABA standards](https://www.access-board.gov/aba/chapter/ch10/): outdoor benches and clear spaces. Existing ground/furniture/light families retain their own cited construction.
