# Form and construction review

This is the requested stop before look-dev. Every review render imports the
published glTF. Quick color, neutral roughness and two original diagnostic
images are deliberately provisional. No final gallery or docs image is committed.
The period photo credits, dates and licenses are in `refs/SOURCES.md`.

## Pass 1 — source drawings, then exported construction

**Color and value first.** Roswell's neutral is the concrete path at far left;
Adair's is the small white cap by the swings; the 2005 photograph's is the gray
stair cheek. Linear-channel gains and exact regions are saved by the photo
script. They are imperfect controls, particularly the tiny cap, not gray cards.
The first heroes were too distant and over-dark: most pixels described an empty
lawn and sky. The equipment red/green itself was not bleached. Do not compensate
for poor framing by turning the equipment white.

Viewed every `renders/pass1/` PNG, including all three heroes and plans, pieces,
clamp/deck, entrance, exit, hanger, arch, spiral and scale. Compared against the
three period photos and dated manufacturer installation sheets.

- The hood is a polygonal arch with abrupt bends, rather than molded volume.
  Replace the corner path with a smooth compound arch, retaining real gauge.
- Its offset collars float beside the hood: add real shoulders from hood to lug.
- The exit is a cut cross-section. Add the rolled toe and lower terminal walls;
  keep a flat 11-inch-plus region, not a lip that launches the rider.
- The long chain is too expensive as all true links. Keep five true links by
  each hanger and use original crossed alpha coverage for the long plastisol run.
  The transition/phase still needs inspection in a close view.
- The original plans overlap the swing envelopes with composite space. Move
  the frames until the conservative envelopes separate; do not hide this in a
  camera. The empty area in the first plans is mostly an incorrectly shared
  swing zone. Keep the connected post/deck cluster.
- Tighten ramp infill to 82.6 mm clear, below 3.5 inches. Nine gaps per bay was
  too wide. Supply real lower and side closures around the tic-tac-toe drums.
- The pieces sheet needs labels and substantially more pixels. The adult-only
  scale image did not show the requested child: fix the copied marker transform.

## Pass 2 — mounting, molded mouths and measured layout

**Color and value first.** At fixed sun intensity, exposure -0.25 and closer
40 mm eye-height framing, the red has a readable highlight without a clipped
white slab, the green remains dark, and the underdeck space stays deep. The
uniform fiber still reads like brown clay and the two-color rubber has a stair-
stepped boundary. These are quick materials, not accepted surfacing look-dev.

Viewed the three revised heroes/plans and all individual pass-2 detail/scale
views at native size. The hood now reaches its clamps and the curved lip has a
real volume. Compared the installed stairs and barrier rhythm with Roswell and
the 2005 shaded structure; compared overall adjacency with Adair.

- The 1996/2005 decks now connect, but the inclusive ramp ended at a narrow
  48-inch deck with no adequate turning bay. Add a six-foot platform at its head,
  with corner posts and side barriers, and close the ramp/neck margins. Do not
  claim the narrow spine itself provides a 60-inch turning circle.
- The roof ridge ends poke beyond the apex; tuck their ends into the shell.
- The closest slides remain very clean and some highlights show low section
  density. Smooth geometric form is the current gate; source detail, seams,
  chalking, contact polish and a high-to-low normal bake remain later work.
- The separate swing zone now has a measurable gap to the composite in every
  plan. The perimeter rectangle is conservative; it is not the exact CPSC union
  of component use zones. The large safe sweep explains the swing's spacing.
- The hanging belt is sagged and the bucket has leg openings. The chain's alpha
  phase is too dense against its modeled upper links; double its physical repeat.
- Reuse the library's own fence/light construction and delivered shrub/tree
  geometry. Limit the inclusive site to one large tree so its extra accessible
  platform and shrub still fit the whole-site cap.

## Pass 3 — usable access, surface footprint and export checks

**Color and value first.** Keep the same quick equipment colors and sun. The
reference pair evaluates volume and construction; no claim is made that smooth
quick plastic or untextured fiber is an accepted used-object finish. The rubber
inset now has a clean curving edge rather than grid-cell steps.

The wide rectangular pads still had dead corners in pass 2. The revised border
follows the connected union of the measured zone envelopes, with 180 mm margins
and an entry neck. This removes unused pad corners without squeezing the swing
sweep. The plans, not the tighter heroes, show the whole installation.

The 2013 head platform is 1.8288 m on post centers, giving 1.7018 m nominal clear
between 127 mm posts; the ramp remains six 1.2192 m sections at 1:12. The former
narrow landing is not presented as a wheelchair turning space. The downstream
spine is narrower and does not make every play event wheelchair accessible.

The fire pole's former clamps had no post at their center and it did not extend
60 inches above a six-foot deck. Give it a dedicated support at the correct
48-inch interface and place that unit at an unroofed 2005 deck edge. Keep the
48-inch and 72-inch interfaces distinct.

Photo fits record a crop-specific effective focal length and the equivalent
full-image lens. Tight crops require a much longer effective lens than the
original full photograph; compare projected landmarks and reported residuals,
not that number alone. The Adair frame is an arched three-post design while the
original kit uses A legs: that construction difference remains visible, and its
fit is weaker than the stair comparison. These are not surveyed replicas.

Final visual inspection and measured results are recorded below after the
checkpoint rebuild. Fine normal detail, real fiber/EPDM finishes and the three
ages remain the next stage, by the brief's stop condition.

## Pass 4 — mechanical export gate

**Color and value first.** Keep the fixed provisional palette, exposure and sun;
this pass changes contact and dimensions, not exposure or age appearance.

Final mechanical addendum: the close chain view showed that changing repeat
length alone stretched each coated oval. The two alternating silhouettes now
have a 45 mm link pitch and roughly 24 × 56 mm outer link dimensions, matching
the true upper links at the image's actual physical coverage. Shorten the chain
run to the raised belt attachment / bucket upper rim, not the center sitting
surface. Counts are unchanged. This is construction correction, not look-dev.

The final scale marker also needed moving into open foreground: a correctly
sized child behind the barrier was still useless evidence. The last scale view
places both markers clear of the equipment. Standalone swing assemblies in the
piece sheet are lifted by the beam height so their seats are not buried below
the sheet's review ground; the exported mating origins remain unchanged.


The independent deck-entry check also caught the 2005 chain net and 2013 ladder
placed on empty neighboring grid bays, plus a 48-inch wall beside a 72-inch deck.
Move them to their real matching deck edges. The narrowed check samples only
the actual entry-tongue / chute-floor triangles around the exported interface
socket, rather than allowing an unrelated collision elsewhere to count as
support. Tube entry has its own bolted flange; its nominal barrel is 780 mm
with at least 744 mm vertical bore, leaving room at the sloped section. All
slides remain under 4k. These are real construction corrections caught before
the checkpoint was frozen.

The final color review caught a bad neutral sample in the 2005 photograph: the
small first rectangle fell on yellow handrail (median sRGB 199/165/79), making
the comparison blue. It is now on the gray stair cheek (184/174/179), with
modest channel gains. Neither the model color nor its exposure was changed.
The rotating drum window also gets a lower closure below the symbols' full
rotation envelope, and the wheel/periscope have real rear shafts/bearings
rather than a small air gap at their board mounts.


The last dimensional audit also found the five-rung 72-inch ladder too widely
spaced. It now has seven rungs, with imported rises of about 261 mm; the 48-inch
unit retains five at 244 mm. Extend the 24-inch slide's level tail so its last
flat region measures 354 mm rather than less than 11 inches. Move the fire-pole
axis 60 mm toward the deck: actual imported reach is 494.5 mm, with 1.656 m
above that platform. These are geometry corrections, not constants reported as
measurements.

Swing clearances now include the beam's 200 mm end overhang and the actual
leg/seat surfaces. Seat-to-leg minimum is 775.5 mm; within-bay seat separation
is 630.0 mm; lateral use-zone reserve is 1.8288 m from the real frame bounds.
Each front/back reserve is 4.8768 m, exceeding twice the imported bearing height
above the lowest nearby depressed surface (4.627–4.788 m). Shift the three frames
250 mm outward and expand the surface accordingly. Composite-to-swing envelope
gaps are now 254 / 191 / 283 mm. All three plans visibly retain contiguous
protective surfacing rather than scattered equipment on an empty rectangle.

### Final inspection and limits

Opened the three final 40 mm / 1.65 m heroes, all three zone plans, the labeled
piece sheet, every construction detail, scale image, actual-mesh sections and
all three photo pairs. Whole-frame display luminance p1 / p50 / p99 is
.006 / .478 / .915 (101), .022 / .475 / .895 (202), and .137 / .693 / .915
(303), with zero fully clipped pixels in each. These describe framing and
render values, not measured material albedos. The inclusive hero has more bright
sky/rubber and still has a hard distant lawn horizon. Its cavities remain dark.

The actual exported slide sections now show continuous trough floors at three
stations; the tube preserves its inner/outer wall. They also expose the tube's
overlapping exit sleeve/runout, which needs a unified high-detail junction.
The entrance close-up still shows a small cheek intersection through a chute
wall; the chain close-up exposes crossed-card transition fins and flat response.
These are known weaknesses, not accepted final geometry/material polish.
Smooth low-section highlights, identical clean coatings, the clay-like fiber and
the sparse horizon also remain CG tells. Do not carry those into final look-dev.

The comparison residuals are 17.0 px for Roswell's deck, 33.8 px for Adair's
swings and 7.9 px for the 2005 stair, in each crop's native source pixels.
Adair's arched uprights differ from the A frame, and its shaded exposure is not
reproduced by the common daylight. The stair is the strongest camera fit;
the deck pair establishes the post/collar/deck relationship but does not copy
the source's decorative infill. Neither a small residual nor serialization
agreement proves photographic realism.

Imported-entry triangle tests confirm 5 / 5 / 7 fastened deck interfaces in the
three sites. Sampled swing sweeps pass at -55…55 degrees in 11-degree increments;
only the bearing articulation is an allowed contact. Other retained controls
have not had a complete dynamic interaction/deformation audit. UVs are
provisional tiled coordinates; final packing, density, bake and age treatment
remain outside this checkpoint. No kit code or existing delivery was changed.

The exact-repeat test initially found two differing bytes per older site, both
in the same reused RedMaple trunk tangent component (-.9714 versus -.9715);
positions, normals, UVs, indices and layout were identical. The checkpoint now
omits explicit tangent accessors and lets importers derive tangent space from
normal/UV data. This is recorded rather than weakening the byte-repeat check.
Final normal-map validation must revisit explicit engine tangent conventions.

The corrected export repeat passes: 104 glTF/BIN/image/site-manifest files
are byte-identical in consecutive factory-startup builds. Imported checks pass
for all 40 modules and three sites, including 17 actual entry contacts and 132
sampled swing poses. Protected existing-delivery hashes are unchanged for all
263 files. No final UV packing, normal bake, physical surfacing or standards
certification is implied by these results.
