Additional read-only primary sources (HTML, not downloaded):

- [FHWA freeway shoulder primer, table 4](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/fhwahop16060/ch2.htm), FHWA 2016, public domain: reproduces AASHTO Green Book cross-section recommendations. Four total lanes: 12 ft lanes, 10 ft right, 4 ft left shoulder.
- [FHWA clear-zone explanation](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/programadmin/clearzone.cfm), FHWA, public domain: explains AASHTO Roadside Design Guide; 1:4 recoverable and 1:3 traversable limits, no universal clear-zone width. The full copyrighted Green Book and Roadside Design Guide were not available locally; no claim to having read their full text.
- [TxDOT ramps and direct connectors](https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/des/rdw/chapter-15-grade-separations-and-interchanges-/15-7-ramps---direct-connectors-.html), TxDOT: speed-change-lane measurement, 300 ft minimum gap-acceptance segment. Authored 366 m acceleration lane plus 91.44 m taper; design illustration, not a traffic engineering certification.
- [FHWA doweled JPCP study, 2009](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/research/infrastructure/pavements/06106/chap1.cfm), public domain: dowels transfer load across joints; our exposed patches mark retrofit slots, hidden bars are omitted.
- [FHWA full-depth repair design](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/pavement/concrete/full3.cfm), public domain: repairs end at joints where possible; repair locations in this generator are placement-owned.
- [WSDOT 1999 bridge barrier memorandum](https://www.wsdot.wa.gov/eesc/bridge/designmemos/08-1999.htm), WSDOT: 32 in F-shape major-highway bridge rail.
- [WSDOT archived barrier plan library](https://wsdot.wa.gov/engineering-standards/all-manuals-and-standards/plan-sheet-library/traffic-barrier-tb), WSDOT: Type 31 introduced in 2009; older installations retain earlier rail heights. This family depicts maintenance in 2012.
- [TxDOT bridge standards](https://www.dot.state.tx.us/insdtdot/orgchart/cmd/cserve/standard/bridge-e.htm), TxDOT: prestressed I-girder construction. Our 54 in generic AASHTO Type IV section and 24 m spans are authored using the traditional section envelope, not the later Tx54 profile or a load-rated design.
- [GDOT sound barrier standard plans](https://mydocs.dot.ga.gov/info/gdotpubs/ConstructionStandardsAndDetails/Forms/AllItems.aspx), GDOT, N-3 dated 23 March 2009: post/panel construction and foundations. Panel span and height here are explicitly authored.
