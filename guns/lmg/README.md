# Propane Defense Harrow 56

Original scripted **belt-fed 5.56×45 light machine gun** visual game asset.
Fixed molded stock, ribbed lower handguard, stamped receiver and feed assembly,
folding carrying handle and bipod, detachable 200-round box and individual
cosmetic belt rounds and links. All geometry is generated by our scripts.
The maker, model and markings are fictional.

**Look round 2, for review.** Original issued finishes, a 4096 Cycles bake and
one canonical 2048 PBR set now accompany the accepted form and animation.
There are no docs JPGs yet. Dense furniture molds and manufactured surfaces
remain original scripted bake sources.

## Finish, wear and texture delivery

Look round 2 replaces the implausible shared paint input for this gun only.
Black enamel starts at linear RGB `.022/.023/.024` (2.2–2.4% diffuse reflectance),
roughness `.42` on the receiver and `.29` on the cover, specular factor `.8`. Phosphate starts at `.028/.029/.030`,
roughness `.57`, metallic `.35`; the bipod retains its lighter `.044/.045/.042` bath color. Original fine conversion grain and
restrained bath mottling remain. Local heat darkens the chamber/gas-seat neighborhoods
toward `.007/.006/.0045`; it does not wash the entire barrel with noise.

The molding inputs are handguard `.006/.0068/.0078`, stock `.009/.0095/.010`,
grip `.032/.034/.036`, box `.023/.024/.023`, and handle `.009/.0095/.010`.
Each retains its own roughness and localized hand/shoulder/impact response.
Material-specific fine molding skin uses 1000–1800 cycles/m, 6–25 µm
source relief and .04–.10 roughness variation (stock .06). Rubber uses
12 µm relief; phosphate also uses 12 µm. These are source parameters, not claims that subtexel detail survives
unchanged. Fine manufacturing reflectance varies ±6% with the molded skin. The close pairs and actual exported normal-map samples judge the bake.

Edge chips expose diffuse phosphate gray first (`.075/.078/.078`, roughness
`.66`, metallic `.12`). Only sharp high points reach bright steel (`.50/.52/.53`,
roughness `.35`, metallic `1`). Cover-top gear scratches and rivet crowns are
independent sites. Brass, copper-jacketed green tips and the accepted form/rig
are retained. All mesh and texture inputs are original scripted fields;
photographs enter only camera/light calibration and comparison measurements.
