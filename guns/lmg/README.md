# Propane Defense Harrow 56

Original scripted **belt-fed 5.56×45 light machine gun** visual game asset.
Fixed molded stock, ribbed lower handguard, stamped receiver and feed assembly,
folding carrying handle and bipod, detachable 200-round box and individual
cosmetic belt rounds and links. All geometry is generated by our scripts.
The maker, model and eventual markings are fictional.

**Form round 2, before look-dev.** One untextured inspection material carries
the original quick colors as vertex attributes. There is no baked normal map,
accepted finish, production atlas or final docs JPG. Dense furniture molds and
manufactured detail sources remain in `High_Sources` for the later normal bake.

## Dimensions and photographic measurements

The accepted envelope remains **1,040 mm** and the barrel exterior span
**465 mm**. Published fixed-stock class references support that approximate
length and the selected long-barrel option. The **7.5 kg** brief target is
nominal; this visual mesh is not a mass model.
[Sources and licenses](refs/SOURCES.md) distinguish published specifications,
photographic estimates and our original design dimensions.

`build/measurements.json`, `build/form-round2.json` and `build/sections.json`
measure the surfaces, separately from export serialization.
`build/reference-measurements.json` records native photographic pixels, rulers
and uncertainty. [The annotated stock ruler](renders/checkpoint/photo-measurements.jpg)
uses the white backdrop as the estimated neutral; that backdrop clips and
cannot calibrate albedo. The open-cover field photo, red sight backdrop and
transparent box image supply form evidence only.

The fixed-polymer stock follows the deep, recessed version in the sharp side
photographs, **without a folding shoulder rest**. Its optional owner remains
in the rig for compatibility. The ribbed handguard follows the older ribbed
exterior reference. This original asset combines class features rather than
reproducing one factory variant.

The near-leg photograph estimates a retracted hinge-to-pad length of 235–260 mm.
Primary class manuals confirm telescoping adjustment but do **not** give individual
leg lengths; the two measured lengths above are not presented as manufacturer specifications.


Measured wall gauges at broad lands are receiver **1.40 mm**,
cover **1.70 mm**, lower handguard **3.16 mm**
and box **4.27 mm**. These are mesh measurements, not published
manufacturer subpart specifications. The receiver has **14 exterior rivets per
flank**; their measured X/Y/Z centers are individually recorded in
`build/form-round2.json`. The upper pressed bosses and rear doublers locate
the groups, with the covered left well and its closed flap beneath the tray.
The receiver's finite folded wall has its embossing continuing inside.
The cover has pressed side walls, flange returns, sight housing, latch, a broad
underside arm and separate pawls. The tray is a finite plate with a smaller
central relief and formed lips. Tiny bevels and fine relief remain dense in
the sources for the eventual normal bake. The handguard has flat sides and a
flat lower land, with three longitudinal ribs. The stock is a changing-section
subdivision shell with an integral shallow side depression, not an extruded
side profile. Its inner wall is visible in both transverse cuts.

The bipod has open channel legs, separate telescoping extensions, finite
pressed pads and a shaped yoke below the gas group. Pads are oriented to lie
nearly flat when deployed. The box has a taller, shallower rounded shell,
molded ribs, a hinged lid, catch and suspension clip. Its lid supports that
clip; there is no unsupported block above a central lid hole.

The sharp loose rear sight photograph gives scale ratios, not a published
subpart drawing. The camera pair retains its entire rim with an explicit
inspection margin and reports the resulting residual. It does not establish
an exact contour match by itself. The whole-gun references likewise differ
in sights, furniture and barrel spacing; the accepted layout remains intact.

## Eye, rig and clips

Meters; palm origin; Blender **+Y forward, +Z up, +X right**; glTF **−Z
forward, +Y up**. Root stays identity. Whole-instance motion belongs to the
consumer. [Interface supplement 1.8.0](INTERFACE.md) is additive.

**64 bones, 12 sockets and 19 clips remain.** Open-bolt, automatic-only display,
nonreciprocating charging handle, folding/extending bipod, detachable box and
count-aware cosmetic belt. Reload lifts the train before removing the box;
ReloadEmpty also draws and returns the charging handle. FireLast finishes
forward and empty; CeaseFire finishes caught at the rear. Visibility is STEP.
The inherited clip schedule and belt guide are unchanged.

`Socket_Eye` is **85 mm behind the actual aperture rim**, on the line through
the front post. [The cheek-weld evidence](renders/checkpoint/eye-weld.jpg)
measures an approximately **88.5 mm** projected station in a supported weld;
**±25 mm** covers the hidden pupil and affine perspective approximation.
The aiming eye is behind the near spectacle lens. This is an inferred eye
plane tied to a measured photographic cheek/hand station, not an anatomical
fit certification. The aimed camera uses that socket directly, with a 20-mm
presentation lens (plus a separate 40-mm sight macro) and no camera-position offset.

Seventeen rounds and sixteen links share one round mesh and one link mesh.
Two round owners and three link owners at the box end have no mesh and cannot
collide. The terminal retained single cuff has a real round around its axis.
The underlying nineteen-slot count/streaming helper remains compatible. The
consumer must occlude its box-end recycling region. This is an authored curve,
not a chain dynamics or retention-force model.

This is an **inert visual asset**. No operative chamber, locking surfaces,
firing pin or gas port is supplied. Two rectangular animation witnesses now
seat at the ready position and separate in the forward-empty pose; they do
not constitute a working sear. Ammunition has solid decorative bases. Checks
prove visual contact, cuff/round association and animation continuity, not
weapon function or fabrication suitability.

The full source and fresh-import sweeps sample every clip at each authored
60-Hz frame, with endpoints and authored keys included. They skip no clip
poses. Pair-relative caching avoids repeating identical solid tests. Ordinary
cross-owner penetration is bounded by 0.2 mm; the two named splay shoes retain
the existing explicit 0.4-mm seating limit. This is sampled clearance, not a
continuous collision guarantee. Source/import affine and surface agreement
prove serialization separately from these geometric fit checks.

Ready witnesses are measured at every ready clip endpoint, alongside the
cover and box latch seats, bipod detents, round stop and pawls. A forward-empty
negative check requires the ready surfaces to separate. The coplanar-contact
fixture rejects a deliberately lifted land. The imported link audit fits
actual cuff circles and round axes, checks all sixteen single cuffs, and shows
the fifteen neighboring interleavings in a real triangle section.

Final delivery remains gated: one 4096 bake downsampled to a **2048 BaseColor /
OpenGL Normal / ORM** set; loaded/empty boxes at lossless 1024 subsets and
cartridge/case/link at 512 through the kit's `texture_size` path. No such maps
or lossless subsets are claimed at this form checkpoint. Fictional marking
masks are prepared but unbaked.

## Rebuild

Requires the installed Blender 5.2, Python/Pillow/NumPy, Noto Sans, GNU make
and flock. Cycles uses the inherited shared GPU lock; scratch stays in `build/`.
No packages, 3D assets, textures or HDRIs are downloaded.

```sh
make -C guns/lmg
make -C guns/lmg check
make -C guns/lmg sections pairs strips extra dimensions evidence review-all
```

Outputs, reference photographs and review images stay ignored. No docs JPGs
are committed. This round changes only `guns/lmg/`; `kit/`, the two existing
5.56/common modules and other gun folders are unchanged.
