# Rill 39 — form round 2 review

Form gate only. Exported GLBs use inspection constants and an original
geometric normal bake, not final materials/wear. Source pairs are labeled
separately. Checked items record observed criteria, not lead approval.
Accepted receiver/furniture sections, side proportions, magazine body/feeds/
path, ammunition and interface meanings were preserved. Selector pose angles
were refined for its wider blade.

## Critique passes

The first checkpoint had six local passes, but lead review correctly rejected
its block-built sights, wire-like guard, long trigger and simplified controls.
Those judgments supersede the earlier local acceptance claims.

1. **Color/value first:** kept inspection constants; pale wood and metallic
   card reflections remain outside material acceptance. Rejected the first new
   sight forgings' stepped shoulders and round guard section. Rebuilt both
   sight bodies as changing rounded sections with smooth remeshed unions,
   retaining dense originals. Made a broad flat guard/folded housing and
   reduced the trigger to compact photographed proportions.
2. **Color/value first:** compared balanced museum gray and sharp display-paper
   references. Rejected a thick slider, straight release tab and missing leaf
   detail. Added the long leaf, thin saddle, two release buttons, spring and
   graduated high sources. Opened the front hood, exposed post detail, and
   developed the selector's widening blade, hub, bent tab and detent. Added
   receiver pin/stamping and grip-ridge sources, then baked their normal.
3. **Color/value first:** Cycles macros exposed black grip projection streaks,
   weak receiver relief and excess trigger depth. Fixed cages/neutral UV regions
   and narrowed the blade's back profile. Replaced the museum trigger's remote
   camera anchors with local guard anchors. Recorded separate sparse photo
   measurements, and revised sight cameras against local landmarks.
4. **Color/value first:** exported macros exposed coincident cover-cap glints
   and a hub buried beneath the selector blade. Inset the cap and extended the
   hub. Strengthened integrated cover ribs while preserving side proportions.
   Surface tests exposed missing front-lug cradle/carrier shoulder contacts;
   added visible seating surfaces and swept the unchanged rock path. Removed
   hidden bolt/hammer render proxies, retaining their transform tracks.
5. **Color/value first:** final macros retain the inspection-finish caveat.
   The front forging still shows some lower-shoulder faceting under strong
   raking light; graduations are quieter than the reference's contrasting
   filled marks. Final differences are listed below; camera residuals and
   contact statistics are not used as visual acceptance.
   Rejected the rear side pair's falsely elevated camera and a clipped selector
   crop; switched rear side to independent receiver anchors and widened the
   selector view. Opened the corrected source/export pairs, all 22 beauty views,
   all 24 photo views, 20 extra stills and all 290 strip frames on 17 inspection
   pages, plus sections, dimensions, trigger observations and contact diagnostics.
   Source/export silhouette and geometric-normal detail agree in these views.

## Independent contacts, visibility and serialization

`build/check.json` and `build/serialization.json` record 44 contact/visibility
groups in source and fresh GLB. These measure evaluated triangles, surface rays
and mesh inventories. Serialization agreement is reported separately.

- Front lug/slot: every half-frame through rock-out 0–38 and rock-in 112–132,
  both reloads; detached between these phases. Rear catch: seated holds at
  0, 1, 8, 10, 138–140, released during removal. Diagnostics show both joints.
- Selector dimple seats in all detents. Safe coverage uses 71 rays across the
  actual handle-neck swept section at receiver skin: 68 hit selector, three
  closed handle at the front end. This samples an area rather than a centerline.
- Top case/lip support is checked on both sides, including vertical outer-surface
  separation. Every visible-round deformation equals Magazine through all 1,556
  semantic poses, preserving these contacts throughout motion.
- Carrier face contacts the front insert shoulder at every clip's end.
- Bolt-lug and hammer/sear render surfaces are absent: zero meshes on those
  bones and no corresponding lug/sear/proxy meshes in source or fresh GLB.
  Transform bones/timing remain. Absence proves invisibility in every clip and
  camera without relying on a few sampled occlusion views.

These are exterior visual assembly proofs. No pressure-bearing lock, working
sear, chamber or rifling, and no functional internal contact claim.

All 20 clips pass the kit half-frame clearance sweep: 1,556 source and 1,568
import samples, zero violations. Ordinary tolerance is 0.2 mm. Four named
visual pin/handle/catch joints have finite bounds; maximum imported overlap
is 1.97104 mm at the inert catch within its 2.1 mm bound. No blanket skip or
global 2.1 mm tolerance is used.

There are 1,556 source/import affine samples: maximum position delta
0.0000913 mm, linear-matrix delta 3.28e-7, evaluated posed-surface delta
0.0000894 mm. Raw normals are finite/unit; minimum triangle/corner dot 0.0916864.
No nonmanifold source parts. All detached GLBs fresh-import; full-frame
