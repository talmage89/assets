# Rill 39 — form round 3 review

Form checkpoint, before finish acceptance. Every pair has a matching image in
`source-pairs/`; the primary stills load the exported GLB. Inspection constants
and a geometric normal remain in use. They do not reproduce worn paint,
phenolic color, wood, filled graduations or oil. No docs JPGs are committed.

The accepted stock/handguard/receiver sections, magazine and rocking path,
ammunition, selector shape, rivets and dimples are retained. A baseline audit
compares coordinates/topology with round 2: 55 accepted meshes are identical;
the two interior cradle bridge ends are intentionally recessed. Rest bones,
20 action tracks and eight socket transforms are identical. The blade's
accepted length, inner curve and forward concavity remain; only its section
tapers toward the toe. Hidden bolt/hammer render geometry remains absent.

## Round 3 critique passes

Earlier self-review missed the sight construction and trusted camera residuals
too readily. The lead's round 3 critique supersedes those acceptance claims.

1. **Color/value first:** the constant grip/steel remain much cleaner and more
   uniform than the references. Kept finish behind the gate. Found sharp
   component photographs and recorded native sizes, credits and estimated
   neutrals. Tan cardboard and the colored work surface have no reliable
   neutral and are form-only. Replaced the tall sight block, wide leaf,
   hexagonal drum caps, heavy hood hooks and separate gas-block ball/box.
   Rebuilt cover flanks/ribs and molded grip panels. Retained dense originals.
2. **Color/value first:** inspection finish still exaggerates large smooth
   highlights. Workbench/Cycles previews exposed boolean triangulation fans.
   Remeshed the cut forgings before game reduction and projected their dense
   surfaces. Rejected reversed grip handedness, tall hood ears, loose grip
   outline and side cameras that rotated into three-quarter views. Fixed
   observed side/rear directions and added fifteen local orthographic views.
3. **Color/value first:** geometric-normal inspection caught inward-facing
   knurl triangles on one button; corrected winding and asserted outward
   faces. A source sweep caught two intersections beneath the lowered sight.
   Raised the spring 0.8 mm and set the saddle's rear edge 1 mm beyond the
   accepted carrier seating face. No clearance tolerance/exception changed.
   All 44 source contacts and all source clips then passed.
4. **Color/value first:** framing previews exposed a partly obscured drum
   circle and clipped component extents. Tucked the thin hood's lower bend
   inside the drum ends, retained a constant normal-offset sheet section,
   and widened the side/above comparison frames. Exported gas views then
   exposed a depression where the upper neck volumes joined. Removing the
   buried cavity alone did not fix it; replaced the union with one smooth
   continuous forging cage and checked side/top/oblique views again. A final
   support audit found the rear spring floating from both saddle and leaf.
   Flared its folded heel onto the saddle lands and raised its middle to meet
   the leaf, keeping the accepted piston envelope clear. A preview sweep
   caught a 0.34 mm piston intersection in the first heel; split its broad
   faces so the folded edges could drop independently of the center. The
   corrected spring passed every clip without adding triangles or exceptions.
   Local orthographic cameras isolate each region and its seating parts to
   prevent other assemblies from obscuring widths. A low RMS is never used
   as contour acceptance.

## Measurements and framing limits

12.9 ±2.1 mm; crosspiece 27.3 ±4.4 mm; hood sheet 1.3 ±0.5 mm and depth

record the baselines, authors, licenses and limitations. The sharp side photos
resolve construction; the Commons macro and installed side establish scale

whole-component frame. Some old whole-rifle/cover crops have much smaller
native detail and remain contextual evidence. All camera residuals are in
`build/cameras.json`; they do not certify a contour between anchors.
