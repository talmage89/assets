nonfunctional visual proxies, without manufacturing dimensions or tolerances.

- [Published class specifications](https://gunrf.ru/rg_avtomat_akm_eng.html):
  880 mm overall, 415 mm barrel and 30-round magazine; historical identification
  information, consulted in browser. No engineering instructions used.
- [Museum catalog](https://www.excitapolonia.pl/en/museum-of-technics/department_small_arms/akm):
  independent 415 mm barrel; mass varies with configuration.
- [Small Arms Survey identification sheet](https://doczz.net/doc/345420/kalashnikov-akm---small-arms-survey):
  secondary mirror of an identification sheet, 3.06 kg unloaded; its 896/436 mm
  length convention differs from the 880/415 class convention. Kept as a
  discrepancy, not used as a fabrication reference.
