- [Published class specifications](https://gunrf.ru/rg_avtomat_akm_eng.html):
  880 mm overall, 415 mm barrel and 30-round magazine; historical identification
  information, consulted in browser. No engineering instructions used.
- [Museum catalog](https://www.excitapolonia.pl/en/museum-of-technics/department_small_arms/akm):
  independent 415 mm barrel; mass varies with configuration.
- [Small Arms Survey identification sheet](https://doczz.net/doc/345420/kalashnikov-akm---small-arms-survey):
  secondary mirror of an identification sheet, 3.06 kg unloaded; its 896/436 mm
  length convention differs from the 880/415 class convention. Kept as a
  discrepancy, not used as a fabrication reference.

Only image/PDF rows above are downloaded by `make refs`. Reference materials
remain ignored. Each native neutral rectangle and linear white-balance gain
is recorded by `src/reference.py`; no color measurement precedes that stage.

Round 3 scale observations and their limitations are recorded in
`../REFERENCE.md` and `../src/form_observations.py`. Sharp standalone side/rear
views are the primary component comparisons. The old 630 px muzzle view and
small museum crops remain contextual evidence only.

The full-frame wear round reuses `early.jpg` for issued wood, handling grime
and magazine/receiver wear, `museum.jpg` for the used receiver's gray exposed
edges and `cover-used.jpg` for the cover's interrupted rim/crest abrasion.
Their authors/licenses/direct URLs remain the rows above. The early view is
balanced on the unprinted cloth below the receiver; the museum on its gray
backdrop. They are compared at whole-gun size as well as in the existing
matched pairs. No new reference pixels enter any material or bake.
