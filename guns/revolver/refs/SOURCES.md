Additional published baseline: https://ojp.gov/pdffiles1/Digitization/105573NCJRS.pdf Appendix C p74 lists the 4-inch six-shot stainless DA/SA class at 40.6 oz unloaded.

Eye placement: https://www.trngcmd.marines.mil/Portals/207/Docs/wtbn/MCRP%203-01B%20-%20Pistol%20Marksmanship.pdf (Pistol Marksmanship) describes arms extended in the standing pistol stance. Ishigaki H, Edagawa H, Miyao M (1995), “On the focal distance of the eye during sighting in pistol shooting”, Perceptual and Motor Skills 81:191–194, DOI 10.2466/pms.1995.81.1.191, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8532455/, reports a foresight approximately 80 cm from eleven marksmen's eyes. Our 800 mm eye-to-front-blade distance minus our 150 mm sight radius gives 650 mm to the rear notch. This is a visual application of that optical distance, not a universal two-handed anatomical measurement. The article abstract is read online; no copyrighted full text is redistributed.

Ammunition uses the published C.I.P. .357 Magnum exterior sheet. Only exterior visual proportions are modeled; the fictional headstamp is reserved for the later normal bake.
