# PD Meridian Six — form and motion checkpoint

Original medium-frame, six-shot .357 Magnum double-action service revolver,
with a four-inch barrel, full-length underlug, adjustable sights and sculpted
wrap-around rubber grips. Fictional maker/model: **Propane Defense Meridian Six**.
Every mesh is generated by Blender 5.2 scripts, from an empty scene.

**Stopped before look development.** This checkpoint has quick constant
materials, a rig, clips, sockets and inspection GLBs. It has no baked textures,
UV acceptance, final consolidated draw-call claim or finished documentation
images. The planned final delivery is one shared 2048 BaseColor / OpenGL Normal
/ ORM atlas and four GLBs through the kit's animated delivery path.

[Reference decisions](REFERENCE.md), [sources and licenses](refs/SOURCES.md),
[form review](REVIEW.md) and the additive [1.4.0 chamber interface](INTERFACE.md)
are committed. Generated images stay ignored until final acceptance.

## Construction and frame

Meters, grip origin, Blender +Y toward the muzzle, +Z up, +X right; glTF +Y up
and −Z forward. These are the held-gun family's conventions. Whole-instance
stance/recoil belongs outside the rig; internal Root stays identity.

The forging has changing rounded sections, machined cheek flats, a separate
seated sideplate and screws, a closely fitted cylinder window, radiused recoil
boss, body-sized swept hammer slot and an integrated front crane-bearing heel.
The barrel has machined side flats, a rib, a rounded lower nose and independently
bored cone/crown. A narrow left release passage retains the accepted cylinder
swing; full rod enclosure remains unresolved in [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md). Front
and rear sights include their bases, pin, notch, hinge and adjustment heads.

The six-chamber cylinder has long round-ended flutes, stop notches and approach
cuts. Its recessed broad extractor plate fills the chamber webs and bears under
each case rim, with a rear ratchet,
central latch hub and rod. The crane carries a bored yoke, upper bearing and
axle. A separate stop, broad thumb latch, center latch pin, hammer with swept
spur, and correctly concave trigger shoe articulate on their own axes. Only
visible visual-asset internals are supplied; this is not a working firearm model.

Rubber starts as a three-dimensional subdivision cage with changing horizontal
sections, palm/backstrap swell and three finger saddles. The fitted cavity is
voxel remeshed and relaxed; its dense source remains in `build/sculpt.blend`.
The hammer has coarse original diagonal checkering and a visible nose. Fine
molded texture, finer checkering, machining, fictional engravings and
the `PD 357 / 26` headstamp are reserved for the later normal/material bake.

The new `../common/magnum357.py` builds the cartridge and open fired case,
including a jacketed soft-point, separate primer and struck fired cup. A
six-pocket speedloader has individual retaining clips and a moving central
push release. No existing common module, other gun or kit file is changed.

## Dimensions and budgets

The measured checkpoint numbers and exact byte sizes are in
`build/serialization.json`; actual part counts are in `build/triangles.json`.
Round-two source/import rays in `build/form-metrics.json` measure the hammer
body/slot, guard band, blade and cylinder-window running light. Photographic
gauge uncertainty is recorded in [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md).
The design targets approximately 240 mm overall, a 101.6 mm barrel, six chambers
and a nominal 1.13 kg unloaded. Mass is metadata, not a measurement from this
visual mesh. [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md) distinguishes published numbers from
authored visual dimensions and motion estimates.

The rounded rubber changes the overall bounds by less than 0.4 mm from the
handoff; the accepted barrel, cylinder and sight anchors retain their scale.
Measured bounds come from evaluated triangle surfaces; optical/cone distances
come from actual mesh bounds and ray intersections. Neither repeats an authored constant
as a measured result.

The active loaded gun is 30,164 triangles; its unloaded gun geometry is 27,044.
The complete payload also counts every hidden ammunition alternative and
reload presentation object. It has 124 named inspection meshes, 32 bones,
22 clips, 10 sockets, nine quick materials and zero textures.

The complete staged gun stays within **40,000 triangles**, including hidden
live/case alternatives, the incoming six rounds, speedloader and single-round
reload display. No hidden geometry state is excluded to make the count pass.
The cylinder and grip receive the largest allocations. The nine temporary
material slots are inspection constants; final delivery will use one atlas
(about 16 MB of block-compressed GPU texture memory including mip levels;
zero texture memory at this checkpoint).

`Socket_Eye` is approximately **650 mm behind the rear notch**, at the same
height as both sight tops. The cited pistol-sighting study reports roughly
800 mm from eye to front sight; our 150 mm sight radius leaves 650 mm to the
notch. The two-hand posture is an authored application. The exact optical frame has no rise,
shift or hidden geometry. A gray backdrop separates the black sight blade,
notch, topstrap and cylinder; a labeled enlarged pixel crop accompanies it.

## Rig and chamber state

The [1.4.0 supplement](INTERFACE.md) declares no magazine, six stable chamber
IDs, loaded/fired/empty state per chamber, DA/SA, swing-out loading and loader
handoff. It does not edit existing 1.0 or 1.1 meanings or the reserved 1.2/1.3
supplements. All frames use 60 Hz, complete keys and explicit hold boundaries;
visibility is STEP. Fired cases remain in their physical chambers until ejected.

| Assembly | Pivot / motion, Blender mm | Visual range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cylinder | (0,38,48.2), Y | 60° per index, six sequential shot clips |
| Crane | (−8,85,20), Y | 0 to −80°, left swing |
| Extractor | cylinder rear axis | −26 mm Y stroke; star and rod together |
| Trigger | (0,31,23), X | −20° DA; −10° cocked to −15° SA |
| Hammer | (0,10,48), X | +35° cocked |
| Cylinder stop | (4.7,37,24), X | −22° rocker retraction |
| Thumb latch / pin | independent guide frames | +2 mm / −2.5 mm Y |
| Loader push release | loader guide | +2 mm Y at release |

These travel/angle values are authored visual estimates. The clips are Idle;
Fire, Fire2–Fire5 and FireLast; IdleAfter1–IdleAfter6; CockHammer and
FireSingle; IdleCocked; DryFire and its empty holds; Reload; ReloadSingle;
and Inspect. [The interface](INTERFACE.md) lists each duration, event and state.
The per-chamber state survives firing; the last live round leaves six fired
cases rather than opening an action. Reload ejection first clears the cylinder
and extended star axially, then lets the cases fall left/down. The loader
enters, seats, releases and withdraws before the crane closes and the stop rises.

Legacy muzzle/hand/butt/eye references retain their scene-root paths. Inactive
magazine/ejection references are documented explicitly. Added Cylinder,
Speedloader and Round sockets carry their complete bone frames. Live rounds,
fired cases, loader rounds and the single insertion round each have separate
rigid ownership. Source and import tests inspect intermediate frames. A separate comparison
against the preserved handoff proves unchanged bones, sockets, clip keys,
ammunition and speedloader, plus all 3,672 cylinder triangles forward of the
extractor seat. `src/form_revision.py --before <handoff.blend> --after
build/revolver.blend --out build/form-revision.json` repeats that audit in Blender.

Requires installed Blender 5.2, Python/Pillow/NumPy, fontconfig, Noto Sans,
GNU make and flock. Downloads are only registered reference images/PDFs.

```sh
make -C guns/revolver                  # complete form checkpoint, no bake
make -C guns/revolver check            # topology, form gauges, contacts, sweep, round trip, repeat
make -C guns/revolver render           # imported GLB Cycles stills
make -C guns/revolver match source-match triggers
make -C guns/revolver sections strips review
make -C guns/revolver clean            # generated build and renders only
```

Cycles uses the inherited shared `ASSETS_GPU_LOCK` and kit GPU wrapper.
All scratch output stays under this worktree's `build/`. No hand edits,
downloaded model, material, texture or lighting enters the asset. The current
surface tests establish the visual assembly and sampled clearance; affine and
surface comparisons separately establish serialization. Neither establishes
photographic finish. Remaining differences and the final checkpoint proof are
recorded honestly in [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md).
