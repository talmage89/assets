# Bracken L20 reference decisions

The original visual carbine belongs to the 20-inch, straight-grip, top-ejecting
.30-30 class. Sources, authors, licenses and direct image/PDF URLs are registered
in [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md); photographs are never modeling, material
or texture inputs.

- Published class: 508 mm barrel, 965.2 mm overall, approximately 3.1 kg planning
  mass and six rounds in the tube. The Smithsonian independently measures a
  38-inch carbine. Mass is not measured from this hollow visual mesh.
- The C.I.P. drawing supplies the cartridge's exterior dimensions. The flat
  nose, separate lead cap, open fired mouth and fictional headstamp retain the
  accepted visual geometry; no working interior is represented.
- Hmaag's lower right-profile carbine and open left action constrain the
  receiver, bands, stock, lever and supported action poses. Cjp24's .375 example
  is a construction comparison with a different stock/finish. Modern
  angle-eject/rebound manuals are distinguished from the older top-eject and
  half-cock animation binding.
- Rest/open lever photos do not recover the same loop envelope. Preserve the
  accepted 104 x 29 mm opening and compare bar-to-opening ratios. The closed
  photo's inferred 2.09 mm rail scales to 3.40 mm at that opening width; the
  adopted lower rail is 3.30 mm, with a concealed upper relief. Its measured
  bow is about 12.95 mm. Camera recovery is an estimate, not engineering data.
- The independent front-facing sight photo includes an inch ruler. Its ruler
  and blade have different perspective scales. The seller's 0.355-inch blade
  height is the stronger height reference; the photo constrains short ears,
  shallow crescent and small notch. Our blade has different mounting details.
  Its gauge is an authored 1.1 mm coin-thickness estimate.
- The primary used-finish reference is the right/top `open2.jpg`: brown/plum
  oxide, gray contact edges and hand-darkened wood. White balance uses its
  broad exposed bolt flat. The full right view uses unclipped white paper;
  the open left/oblique views use exposed screw/pin facets. The sight photos
  use their gray backdrops; the ammunition photo uses its exposed gray lead.
  Reflected silver and lead are estimates, not gray cards or recovered albedo.
- Wear follows contact: lower receiver corners/edges, lever's inner lower rail,
  gate perimeter, band rims and crown. Wood impacts stay at toe, heel and
  exposed forend margins; hand oils darken the wrist. Review compares 2D worn
  areas, connected regions and both length/height profiles, rather than using
  one line statistic as visual acceptance.

[REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md) records rejected forms/finishes and remaining differences.
The exact bone frames, clips and socket meanings are documented separately in
