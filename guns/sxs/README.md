# PD Covey 26 — boxlock side-by-side shotgun

An original script-built visual game asset: a fictional Propane Defense
12-gauge field shotgun, with 26-inch joined barrels, double triggers,
extractors, a top lever, automatic tang safety, straight-hand stock and splinter
forend. No working lockwork, pressure design or manufacturing model is supplied.

**Form round 7. Finish, wear, markings, checkering and docs JPGs remain held.**
The head now uses turned surfaces and milled lands with distance blends, with
its dense source baked onto the game mesh. The camera and outline gate remains
separate from a passing build. See [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md) for the image critique,
failed comparisons and exact acceptance limits.

The [published class specifications](https://www.ugartecheashotguns.com/shotguns/specifications/)
offer the boxlock, double triggers, extractors, 26-inch barrels and splinter
forend together. Their 38/63 mm comb/heel-drop baseline informs the original
stock. A straight hand keeps the field silhouette lean and permits shifting
the hand between triggers. The slightly longer measured pull is a disclosed
original-design difference. Installed fonts will be found through `kit/fonts.py`
when original markings are added; no real markings or engraving are copied.

The [primary cheek-station instructions](https://www.brownells.com/userdocs/learn/076-200-548_Pull___Drop_Gauge.pdf)
place the cheekbone 1¼–1½ inches behind the comb nose. The accepted station
remains 35 mm behind the nose, at Y=−123 mm. The
[CPSA handbook](https://www.cpsa.co.uk/userfiles/files/CTSH.pdf), printed p. 4-2,
checks a sporting sight picture with a pound coin on the rib 200 mm ahead
of the standing breech: the bead's center just clears the coin. The historical
[round pound is 3.15 mm thick](https://www.royalmint.com/collect/collector-resources/designs-and-specifications/one-pound-coin-designs/).
Extending that line from the actual rib surface and bead back to the accepted
cheek station gives eye Z=60.500 mm, 3.612 mm above the extended rib and
42.811 mm above the actual comb. The local comb drop there is 39.199 mm;
the accepted comb-nose drop remains 38.02 mm. This derives a fitted visual
sight picture; it does not claim an anatomical measurement of a human.

The local primary side-by-side manual supplies its illustrated exterior parts,
double-trigger order, forend iron and controls. Direct sources, photographic
authors/licenses and reference limitations are in `refs/SOURCES.md`.
Photo landmarks calibrate cameras only; no photo enters modeling or materials.

## Head construction and changed measurements

There is no free-form height cage for the head. Each fence is a surface of
revolution around its continued bore axis, with a circular R13.35 front section.
A short convex front crown rolls into an eased hollow fall. The ground sides,
R6 roof/side return, rear plane and broad inclined milled central land meet
through compact C2 signed-distance blends. The central land's R2 edges are
buried beneath the fences. The original R5.90 boss recess and R0.60 lip are cut
into the result. All surfaces and texels are generated by our scripts.

`src/head_surface.py` evaluates that zero set at 0.05 mm along Y and 1,281
transverse angles. `src/head_mesh.py` remeshes it with shared roof/lip vertices.
The dense original remains in `High_Sources`; reduction and the normal bake
supply the game mesh. A coordinate-owned 0.006 mm weld makes close crown
samples deterministic. No image enters geometry, materials or baking.

The front center retains Z55.59 at the rib floor. The rear central plane now
falls 0.32 mm per rearward mm. The former level rear datum stood above the
falling fences. The photo's strongest front highlight and broad rearward fall
motivate this departure and the new turned meridian; brightness is not treated
as a metric height scan. The uncalibrated black-backed macros retain unity
balance. Finish constants are held.

## Cameras and evidence

and the rib line, retaining the original optical center through the crop. The
open trial also fits principal point and barrel/lever pose from a visible rim,
controls, a breech corner and rigid lines. Both actual safety detents and multiple
initial views are tried. The open view has an explicit muzzle-side constraint;
a low-error opposite-side fit was rejected after viewing its render. Neither
head outlines nor joint observations influence a camera fit. Blender independently
checks the pinhole projection, roll and crop shifts.

## Frame, rig and visual assembly

Meters, palm origin, Blender +Y forward / +Z up / +X right; glTF −Z forward /
+Y up. [Interface 1.7.0](INTERFACE.md) retains the 17-bone rig, 19 clips and
14 sockets. Whole-instance movement stays external; Root remains identity.
Front Trigger operates right chamber 0; rear Trigger operates left chamber 1.
The concave trigger faces point toward the muzzle and the tips curl forward.
Hidden hammers are state only. This is exterior visual construction, with no
functional firearm mechanism or pressure design.

angles, 0.5° apart. It is not a continuous-angle certificate.

The gun stays within 39,000 triangles, including staged ammunition alternatives.
One 4096 GPU bake ships at 2048 through the kit finalizer/downsampler/exporter.
BaseColor and ORM are RGB8; the normal retains RGB16 precision through a
normalized vector filter. About 16 MiB with ordinary block compression and
mipmaps; actual uncompressed importer storage depends on format handling.
Other parts retain their GPU normal bake. The forging's dense normals are
projected through its actual Mikk tangent basis, with construction ownership
and exact occupied-texel padding. No photo or painted repair is used.
Standalone shell/hull diagnostic exports retain their accepted dimensions;
small baked standalone sets belong to the later finish gate.

make -C guns/sxs
make -C guns/sxs check
make -C guns/sxs sections match
make -C guns/sxs reflections

flock. All scratch stays in `build/`; every Cycles bake/render uses the shared
GPU lock. No kit/shared-common changes, downloaded code or final docs images.
Reference URLs, authors, licenses and uncertainty remain in
