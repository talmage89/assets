# Original form plan

Propane Defense **Covey 26**: a boxlock side-by-side 12-gauge field shotgun.
26 in / 660.4 mm barrels, 1080 mm overall, 365 mm front-trigger pull target,
38 / 63 mm comb/heel drop class, nominal 3.2 kg (not simulated).

A straight-hand walnut stock permits the hand to shift between the double
triggers and keeps the original silhouette lean. A slim splinter forend leaves
the joined barrels visible. The planned finish is blued barrels/action rather
than case color: the plain used field-gun references support it and it makes
construction and localized thinning legible without decorative color. Finish
is held until the form checkpoint is reviewed.

The game asset shows exterior assembly and visible cavities. Its internal
lockwork is absent; cocking is state in the interface. Motion ranges and
clearances are authored visual estimates, never manufacturing specifications.
Dimensions come from evaluated surfaces; published anchors and photographic
estimates are labeled separately. Photo landmarks calibrate cameras only.

The form gate includes all staged live/fired/reload alternatives in the
25–35k budget. Dense changing-section wood cages remain as bake sources.
Final plan: one 2048 gun atlas; separate small shell and hull atlases.

Look-dev remains held. Its named wear sites will be: broad satin thinning on
the barrels' carry area, narrow gray-blue muzzle rub, faded handled action
flats with softer exposed edges, polished thumb contact on the lever, a smooth
guard underside, flattened checkering diamonds at the wrist/forend grips,
and compressed pale dents at the stock toe and forend edges. Each effect will
follow its contact/exposure cause and be judged in two-dimensional masks at
hero scale. Whole-part hue/value histograms and highlight color must be
accepted against white-balanced used-gun photographs before baking.
