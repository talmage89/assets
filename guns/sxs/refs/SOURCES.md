Primary published specifications read online:

- [Manufacturer class specifications](https://www.ugartecheashotguns.com/shotguns/specifications/):
  boxlock, 12 gauge, 2¾-inch chamber, 26-inch barrels, double triggers,
  extractors and English splinter forend are offered together. Stock baseline:
  365 mm pull, 38 mm comb drop, 63 mm heel drop, straight or pistol grip.
  These establish the class, not a copied product.
- [Manufacturer manual index](https://eaacorp.com/owners-manuals/):
  identifies its primary original boxlock manual, https://online.fliphtml5.com/fzoyl/akrk/ .
  Its external-control descriptions establish the front/right trigger and
  tang-safety direction. PDF mirrors returned 404, so they are not build inputs.
  The historical manual at
  https://www.remarms.com/sites/default/files/2026%20Revised%20Historical%20OMs/Model%20SPR%20210%20%26%20220%20%28REV%200426%29.pdf
  was read through its indexed text, but repeated direct retrieval returned
  HTTP 522. The local primary side-by-side document supplies illustrated assembly.
  Original gun targets 1080 mm overall and 3.2 kg; mass is an unmeasured design
  class, not mesh-derived mass.

The shell repeats the accepted pump shotgun's original exterior visual design:
22.4 mm rim, approximately 20.1 mm hull, 57.5 mm closed and 69.8 mm open.
The shared module is independent of the pump's sources/build outputs. Nominal
2¾-inch shell length describes the opened hull. No working chamber, pressure
design, firing mechanism or manufacturing drawing is supplied.

Eye placement is revised in round 2; see the fitting evidence and its stated
limitation below. No human anatomical measurement is claimed.

## Fitting evidence and scope

Brownells' primary [Pull & Drop Gauge instructions](https://www.brownells.com/userdocs/learn/076-200-548_Pull___Drop_Gauge.pdf),
2005, p. 2, place the cheekbone 1¼–1½ inches (31.75–38.10 mm) behind
the comb nose, and describe the field eye relative to the sighting plane.
Read online; direct image/PDF fetch returned HTTP 403, so no local PDF is claimed.
The midpoint 34.9 mm locates our visual cheek station behind the nose.
This is a **fore/aft cheek measurement**, not an anatomical eye-height measurement.
No retrieved source measures one shooter's cheek-to-pupil vertical distance.
The socket's vertical fit remains an authored visual fit and is reported as such.
The old 45 mm assertion is withdrawn as evidence of a measured cheek weld.

The sharp 12-gauge dealer set has a 28-inch barrel, 14¾-inch pull with pad,
1¾-inch comb drop and 3 1/16-inch heel drop. It supplies construction, not our
accepted stock drops. The manufacturer class specification above supplies
38 / 63 mm drops. The separate [plain imported class listing](https://www.gunsinternational.com/guns-for-sale-online/shotguns/ugartechea-shotguns/ignacio-ugartechea-boxlock-side-by-side--12-ga--27-75--barrels.cfm?gun_id=103275586)
reports 1¾ / 2½-inch drops and a solid rib taper of 8.5 → 4.5 mm. Its photos
returned 403 and are not used for acceptance.

Round-2 image-measurement limits: the photographed parts exceed 600 native
pixels in the selected macros, but they have no ruler or calibrated camera.
Wrist and splinter measurements reported in the README are evaluated model
sections. Their exact millimeter agreement with these photos is not proven.
The eight view-locked macro fits preserve the observed side of each part while
fitting translation, focal estimate and roll; residuals are retained, not
minimized by orbiting to a different side.

Construction of the visual eye: sample the actual rib at Y=315 mm, 200 mm in
front of the Y=115 mm standing breech. Raise that point by 3.15 mm. Continue
the line through the bead center (Y=766, Z=54.7 mm) back to the accepted
Y=−123 mm cheek station. The evaluated rib yields Z≈54.492 mm at the coin,
so the eye is Z≈60.500 mm. The actual comb there is Z≈17.689 mm: eye above
comb ≈42.811 mm. Equivalently, the local comb drop ≈39.199 mm plus the fitted
eye's ≈3.612 mm above the extended rib gives ≈42.811 mm. The comb-nose drop
remains ≈38.02 mm; the cheek station is 35 mm farther back on the sloping
comb. This derivation replaces round 2's unsupported 49.31 mm human offset.
It represents this visual asset's fitted field view; no human mesh or personal
fit is certified.

The original rib dipped abruptly beyond the chambers, so the coin rule hid
its forward surface. Its sighting surface now follows the straight line
between the accepted breech and muzzle heights. Both endpoint sections, the
