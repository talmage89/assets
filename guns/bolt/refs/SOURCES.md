Other primary published class references inspected online:

- [Gunsumer Reports original external-feature review](https://www.gunsumerreports.com/review_remington_700_sps_tactical_aac-sd_p3.php):
  the sharp original photographs above replace the blurred field action as the
  main form evidence. The receiver diameter supplies an approximate local scale:
  the port is about two diameters long, chosen as 72 mm for our visual asset.
  Perspective and the different action prevent a manufacturing-dimension claim.
- [MDT five-round steel magazine specification](https://mdttac.com/products/308-6-5-creedmoor-mdt-metal-aics-magazine-5-rnd):
  3.055-inch outside length and 2.52-inch rear height. Ours uses 77.597 mm length
  and 64.008 mm rear height, with original pressed ribs, catch notch and floor
  lip. Only its external visual class and envelope are used.
- [LimbSaver medium pad specification and templates](https://limbsaver.com/pages/blog-support-library-template):
  medium blank 5-3/32 × 1-13/16 inches (129.38 × 46.04 mm), grindable down to
  4-1/4 × 1-5/16 inches. Our unbranded pad is 127 × 43.5 mm at its full section,
  within that trim range. This checks butt height; it does not copy the outline.

- [Vortex Canada 4–16×44 specification](https://vortexcanada.net/riflescopes/vt-dbk-10026-diamondback-tactical-4-16x44-ffp-30mm-ebr-2c-moa):
  30 mm tube, 357.1 mm length, 96.5 mm eye relief, 44 mm objective. Our optic
  chooses 357 mm length and 88.9 mm relief (the brief's 3.5-inch target).
- [Harris Engineering S-BRM](https://www.harrisbipods.com/product/s-brm-bipod/):
  6–9 inch extension class, 1-inch notches, sling-stud attachment, steel/hard
  alloys and hard rubber feet; legs fold toward muzzle. Our geometry and
  mounting saddle are original.
- [C.I.P. .308 Winchester sheet](https://bobp.cip-bobp.org/uploads/tdcc/tab-i/tabical-en-page125.pdf):
  use the published case/cartridge envelope (51.18 / 71.12 mm, 12.01 mm rim)
  for the visual ammunition. No loading or manufacturing data is implemented.

The filenames `left.jpg`, `left_sps.jpg`, and `left_precision.jpg` are retained
cache names; those photographs show the right side. `left_standard.jpg` supplied
the round-one left comparison; no photograph is mirrored.
Its own EXIF records 5.7 mm focal length and **35 mm equivalent**. The round-one camera
used that known equivalent lens with a 3069/3005 horizontal crop correction;
only its pose is fitted. Other photo focal lengths remain estimates.

The 13.5-inch pull, five-round detachable magazine and near-vertical grip are
brief/design targets. Mass is a design-class target, not inferred from mesh
volume. Bolt travel, control angles and all internal interfaces are visual prop
approximations, not functional engineering or manufacturing dimensions.

Round 2 measurement policy: a photograph gives ratios, with perspective error,
unless a published dimension above supplies the scale. The original guard bow
uses a 14.5 × 7.6 mm rounded rectangular section, estimated from the broad flat
faces in the bottom-metal photo (about 12–15 mm wide and 6–8 mm deep). The bipod
uses 14.5 mm sleeves, 9.5 mm sliding rods and 24.8 mm flat-flared feet; these are
photo-scaled visual estimates, not manufacturer-published diameters. Their
relative thickness and cup profile are checked in the new detail renders.
On the 1000-pixel manufacturer photo, the saddle-to-lowest-foot height is about
970 pixels. Using the published 9-inch maximum gives approximately 0.236 mm per
pixel: locally read transverse bands of roughly 35–40 pixels for a sliding rod,
60–65 for a sleeve and 105–115 for a foot flange imply about 8.3–9.4, 14.2–15.3
and 24.8–27.1 mm. These are bounded visual estimates: perspective, rounding and
the maker's generic product photograph limit their accuracy. Notch pitch is
taken from the written 1-inch specification, independently of that photograph.

Round two replaces that cluttered left comparison with the clean PEO Soldier
profile. Its source EXIF supplies 105 mm equivalent; that focal length is fixed
while pose is fitted. Original image padding remains uncertain. Pair captions
show native-pixel residuals and distinguish EXIF from estimated lenses.

The finish review measures explicit polygons in `open.jpg`, a daylight photograph
of a used field rifle. Its upper olive-gray paint, unpainted bridge and exposed
black scope strip are separate regions of the same rifle. Their camera values
include sky, surroundings, exposure and white balance; they are not recovered
albedos. Our paint is original olive spatter, not its camouflage. The supplemental
daylight photo cross-checks the range of the blacks on another used rifle.
`detail_032.jpg` also supplies the single rounded receiver tang beneath the
cocking tail: the accepted action's exposed fork was replaced with a shallow
continuous tongue. No manufacturing or internal mechanism is inferred.

### Reference color correction for the lead's three-fix review

All seven cached photographic references are adapted in linear RGB using an
independently selected neutral before measurement/pair composition. Original
reference bytes are retained. Native neutral/material coordinates and gains
are authored in `src/white_balance.py` and `src/reference_measurements.py`;
`REVIEW.md` records their uncertainty. The right-side bare bipod fastener sets
the primary black-finish balance; shaded Rackandchamber camouflage is auxiliary.
Clipped backdrop whites do not establish an illuminant: the profile uses its
gray mounting ring, and the left reference's residual cast stays unconstrained.
Generated corrections and marked measurement proofs live in ignored
`build/white-balanced/`; photographs never become material inputs.
