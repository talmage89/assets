## Round 1 critique history

These seven passes describe the initial submission. The lead subsequently rejected
the eye view and five form regions; the old author acceptance did not establish
photographic accuracy. The current review and images are below.

1. **Primary volume / orthographic clay.** Color/value was unavailable in clay;
   highlight bands were judged first. The first 44,273-triangle build was over
   budget. Its stock had an exaggerated dip ahead of the comb and the forend
   read too circular. Rejected. Raised and eased the wrist-to-comb transition,
   reduced low-value geometry, retained the dense wood sources and measured
   changing sections rather than trusting the side silhouette.
2. **First Cycles construction pass.** Color/value first: wood was too pale
   under oversized reflection cards, and the metal's cavities lacked separation.
   Corrected inspection color/lighting. More seriously, a rectangular internal
   cut broke through the curved roof, the battery bolt did not fill the port,
   the optical view missed the bead, and the tabletop backdrop blocked a muzzle
   camera. Rejected. Rebuilt the inner roof section, bolt length/pocket, crown
   bead height, actual Eye ray and transparent held-object presentation.
3. **Independent right / left / open photographic pairs.** Color/value first:
   the gray custom-coated right reference is much brighter than our blued steel;
   it cannot set bluing albedo. The left wood sample is more orange, while our
   temporary wood is uniformly brown. Form review found broad triangular normal
   fields on the receiver, an overly long opening and soft hose-like forend
   grooves. Rejected those. Repaired flat/roof normals, shortened the port and
   broadened the forend's carved faces. The original paired groove rhythm and
   fuller wrist remain deliberate design differences.
4. **Actual contacts and slow subframes.** Color/value stayed subordinate to
   geometry: the dark internal values concealed unsupported contact in attractive
   poses. The old flat tray did not cradle the rim; several internal paths crossed
   their supporting metal. Rebuilt the concave tray, visible seats and pockets,
   rim retention, front bar channels and carrier clearance. A follower count
   change interpolated through the departing shell at a half-frame; made that
   occluded reindexing STEP. Every exception remains bounded and measured.
5. **Round trip, aimed view and battery cheek.** Color/value first: roof highlights
   now stay broad and cavities stay dark, but constant wood still lacks grain and
   oil-finish breakup, as expected at this gate. The shell socket collapsed during
   a hidden/visible transition after export. Added a nonscaling guide and tested
   its complete affine frame. Further visible review found an exposed lower bolt
   pocket in battery. Added a supported port cheek that clears the loading tray.
   Reset cap/flat normals and retained analytic roof normals, rather than hiding
   distortion with a rough shader. The final source and imported pairs/strips
   are regenerated from this revision.
6. **Shell and assembly close review.** Color/value first: the quick red hull
   and brass remain too uniform for finish acceptance, but their silhouettes and
   dark interior are legible. The first crimp was obscured by its own backing and
   read as a flat disk. Rebuilt six actual recessed folds, bringing the standalone
   to 596 triangles. Removed a discontinuity in the rear roof taper exposed by
   the aimed view. Finally added the action slide block: the earlier bars and
   bolt followed the same schedule without a visible supporting link. New contact
   checks measure the actual block/bolt bearing throughout the stroke. These
   construction fixes are part of this checkpoint, not deferred texture work.
7. **Final exported raking views.** Color/value first: the temporary wood still
   has a broad, uniform reflection and cannot yet pass as finished hardwood.
   Both new three-quarter views expose its compound volume rather than only
   the side outline. The receiver close-up exposed one triangular highlight at
   the front shoulder. A clamped bevel corner had missed the analytic roof
   normals; repaired the assignment and checked the regenerated close-up and
   both quarters. The roof now rolls through that shoulder without the triangle.
   No silhouette geometry or triangle budget was added for this correction.
