[Woodland engineering standards](https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1082/2021-Engineering-Standards-PDF),
City of Woodland: residential 25 ft cobra mounting and six-foot arm context.
[Woodland street lights](https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/777/Street-Lights), City:
metal/concrete municipal poles versus wooden utility-owned poles.
[Durham street-light program](https://www.durhamnc.gov/5563/Street-Light-Program),
City of Durham: wooden poles and 25 ft LED mounting are another regional norm.
These sources support a municipal metal-pole installation, not a claim that one
pole material is universal across American suburbs.
