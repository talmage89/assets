# Reference and dimensional decisions

A municipal galvanized-steel installation is chosen. [Woodland's street-light
page](https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/777/Street-Lights) distinguishes city metal /
concrete poles from utility-owned wooden poles; its [engineering standards](https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1082/2021-Engineering-Standards-PDF)
include residential cobra heads. [Durham](https://www.durhamnc.gov/5563/Street-Light-Program)
uses wooden poles as its residential norm. Neither material is universal; this
asset represents the former municipal/underground-wiring arrangement.

The manufacturer-authored [Valmont DS210 sheet](https://assets.villalighting.com/docs/1141188_Specsheet.pdf),
row R590A250, supplies a **25 ft / 7.620 m** shaft, **5.90 in / 149.86 mm** base
OD and **2.40 in / 60.96 mm** top OD, 11-gauge wall. Its anchorage row supplies
a **10 in / 254 mm** square plate, **0.875 in / 22.225 mm** thick, **9 in / 228.6 mm**
bolt circle, and four **1 in / 25.4 mm** anchor rods. Embedment is omitted from
the game mesh. The 380 mm round, 60 mm high plinth is an authored above-ground
placement aid, not a structural footing design.

[PBOT P-651](https://www.portland.gov/transportation/engineering/documents/p-651/download)
gives a **6 ft / 1828.8 mm** arm with approximately **18 in / 457.2 mm** rise,
a paired three-bolt flange, pole cap and reinforced handhole construction.
Its 8 × 8.75 in flange envelope informs the original flange. Arm-root height,
end straight, taper, clamp jaw and assembly layout are authored to fit this shaft.
The optical plane is approximately **7.774 m** above this prop's ground datum.
This is a mixed-reference original assembly, not a certified municipal pole SKU.

[Current's compact fixture sheet](https://cdn.currentlighting.com/site/specsheet/OLP3187-Evolve-ERLC-ERL1-ERL2-Gen-5-LED-Roadway-Lighting-Spec-Sheet.pdf),
page 6, gives **489 × 191 mm**, a **108 mm** rear body height and 42–60 mm pipe
mounting range. Page 2's photograph informs the stepped driver hump, front
optical recess, casting split, heat fins and black control. The housing, folded
reflector layout and fasteners are original. The 36 W / approximately 4800 lm
class is a lighting setup reference; no manufacturer's photometric distribution
is reproduced. Our small LED tiles emit through actual transmissive glass.

[Intermatic control sheet](https://djuly1j3idynn.cloudfront.net/userfiles/images/inriver/original/220976_photocontrol_lc4536lac_technical_data_en.pdf)
provides a **69.9 mm** twist-lock housing-height example. The fictional black
control uses that scale, with an authored 76.2 mm diameter and different external
details. Overall prop height with the control is **7.9561 m**. Fixture sheets'
short receptacle/shorting-cap dimensions must not be confused with a fitted
photocontrol's full height.

References are comparison-only. [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md) records files,
authors/rights and derived reading/photograph aids. No manufacturer's marks,
reference texels, model or lighting assets are used.
