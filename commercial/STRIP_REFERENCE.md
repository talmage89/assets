The L's elbow is a genuine 64 × 64 ft rear storage/service volume connected
to the return leg, rather than two overlapping shop rows. The two wing bays
face west; main bays face south. Its upper parking rows stop short of the
covered walk, and a reserved cross aisle connects the front aisle to the
street-side aisle. The matched pickup route passes around the wing instead
of treating the building as an empty plan line.

The grammar checks 1,000 plans for dimensions, parking/aisle overlap and
accessible-space counts. Four actual proof plans receive occupied-lot vehicle
sweeps: a 2.14 × 5.70 m pickup, 4.8 m center-radius arcs, one-degree turns,
and straights at no more than .18 m. A 1.85 × 4.70 m passenger car enters a
standard space and reverses along the same steering path with its neighbors
occupied. This proves those geometric paths, not opposing traffic, emergency
access, delivery-truck turning, tire dynamics or drainage design.

## Photographs and camera limits

- **Texas used shop row, 29 August 2015**, Michael Barera, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  Inspect aluminum framing, shallow transoms, the long sign fascia, glass
  darkness and room depth. A narrow empty-bay window supplies four sparse
  anchors. The photo has a much shallower shelter and a metal fascia rather
  than our freestanding full covered walk. Its warm finish is not copied.
- **California used low center**, Yngvadottir, CC BY-SA 4.0. Description says
  2018, EXIF says 2011; neither is a construction date. Inspect the steep
  mansard, masonry separators, independent signs, shaded glazing and bed.
  Our mansard is asphalt shingles, while the photo's roof is standing-seam
  metal. The material difference is held out, not disguised as a match.

The Texas neutral is white vehicle hatch paint, excluding identification,
glass and lamps. The California neutral is the sunlit gray public sidewalk.
Native ROIs, linear RGB gains and hashes are generated in
`build/strip/references/balance.json`. These are assumed neutrals, not gray
cards or recovered albedos. The kit fits camera pose/lens to four landmarks
per photo and independently checks projection in Blender. Both are weak,
approximately 10 native-pixel RMS type comparisons. Low residual alone
cannot prove their unsurveyed bay widths, our whole plan, or exact finishes.
Procedural sun/sky directions are estimated from the photographs' shadows.

Downloaded reference rights/direct files are in [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md).
They remain inspection inputs and never supply mesh or texture pixels.
