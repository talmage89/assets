The L's elbow is a genuine 64 × 64 ft rear storage/service volume connected
to the return leg, rather than two overlapping shop rows. The two wing bays
face west; main bays face south. Its upper parking rows stop short of the
covered walk, and a reserved cross aisle connects the front aisle to the
street-side aisle. The matched pickup route passes around the wing instead
of treating the building as an empty plan line.

The grammar checks 1,000 plans for dimensions, parking/aisle overlap and
accessible-space counts. Four actual proof plans receive occupied-lot vehicle
sweeps: a 2.14 × 5.70 m pickup, 4.8 m center-radius arcs, one-degree turns,
and straights at no more than .18 m. A 1.85 × 4.70 m passenger car enters a
standard space and reverses along the same steering path with its neighbors
occupied. This proves those geometric paths, not opposing traffic, emergency
access, delivery-truck turning, tire dynamics or drainage design.

## Photographs and camera limits

- **Texas used shop row, 29 August 2015**, Michael Barera, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  Inspect aluminum framing, shallow transoms, the long sign fascia, glass
  darkness and room depth. A narrow empty-bay window supplies four sparse
  anchors. The photo has a much shallower shelter and a metal fascia rather
  than our freestanding full covered walk. Its warm finish is not copied.
  The photographed commercial row is reported as built **2002** by
  [CountyOffice's published property record](https://www.countyoffice.org/live-oak-st-commerce-tx-property-records/).
  This is a secondary public-record aggregator, not independent assessor verification.
- **Virginia center, built 1989**, Bernstein Management Group's September
  2016 [Sterling Village brochure](https://0201.nccdn.net/1_2/000/000/0f2/4b8/3-page-flyer-Sterling-Village-Center_9-16-16--1-.pdf),
  p.1. The same page states a 2015 renovation. Photographer is unnamed and no
  open license is stated; the extracted original JPEG stays in ignored build
  comparisons. A clear right-pavilion window supplies four anchors. Inspect
  the fascia, shelter, glazed height and raised entry punctuation; its hipped
  metal pavilions differ from our flat towers. Cars, stitching and low JPEG
  resolution limit its precision. No site-plan drawing is traced or used.
- **California used low center**, Yngvadottir, CC BY-SA 4.0. Description says
  2018, EXIF says 2011; neither is a construction date. Inspect the steep
  mansard, masonry separators, independent signs, shaded glazing and bed.
  Our mansard is asphalt shingles, while the photo's roof is standing-seam
  metal. The material difference is held out, not disguised as a match.
  A later property-year search indicates an older center. Keep this solely
  as auxiliary vocabulary; it does not establish 1985–2005 construction.

The Texas neutral is white vehicle hatch paint, excluding identification,
glass and lamps. Virginia uses the white painted fascia's lower trim beside
the left pavilion, excluding teal paint/glazing. Its tiny JPEG ROI makes
that balance tentative. An initially selected yellowish sidewalk patch gave
an excessive blue correction and was rejected after inspection. California's
neutral is the sunlit gray public sidewalk.
Native ROIs, linear RGB gains and hashes are generated in
`build/strip/references/balance.json`. These are assumed neutrals, not gray
cards or recovered albedos. The kit fits camera pose/lens to four landmarks
per photo and independently checks projection in Blender. Texas/California
are weak, approximately 10 native-pixel RMS type comparisons. Virginia's
39.2 mm fit is .30 native-pixel RMS on one low-resolution pane; that small
residual does not make its unsurveyed measurements stronger. Low residual alone
cannot prove their unsurveyed bay widths, our whole plan, or exact finishes.
Procedural sun/sky directions are estimated from the photographs' shadows.

Downloaded reference rights/direct files are in [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md).
They remain inspection inputs and never supply mesh or texture pixels.

## Look checkpoint: dated service photographs

The 2007 Westlakes offering memorandum reports construction as 1985 on one
page and 1986 on another: retain that conflict, giving 21–22 years of use.
Two photographs supply the covered-walk and repaired-lot comparisons. They
are type/material evidence, not a survey or a surface whose pixels are
reproduced. Neutral balance uses an asphalt patch in the walk photograph
and a sunlit curb top in the lot photograph. The rejected blue-shadow
asphalt neutral and its excessive correction are recorded in REVIEW.md.

Eric G. Bachman's 2015 rear photograph supplies gray service steel, lower
wall deposits and repaired paving. Its building date is unverified, so it
is an auxiliary construction/age reference rather than dated period proof.
California remains an older auxiliary. Commerce and Sterling plus the two
Westlakes views provide four dated-period comparisons; none establishes our
authored parcel dimensions. The rear uses neutral overcast procedural sky;
the other pairs estimate sun directions from visible shadows.

The neutral ROIs, linear RGB gains, source hashes and fitted residuals are
rebuildable in `build/strip/references/balance.json` and
`build/strip/cameras.json`. Fixed visible-variation measurements, annotated
exclusions and actual exported-surface ray checks are documented in STRIP.md.
The native walk ROI is only about 20 × 4 pixels: its uncertainty is material.

The revised sites retain the cited construction/parking dimensions but
author distinct parcels: 336×244, 390×210, 348×260 and 396×244 ft.
The wide row moves east; the arcade moves west and staggers its islands;
the L uses two street frontages with an east exit. No property plan is traced.
