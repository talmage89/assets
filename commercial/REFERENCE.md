# Commercial / corner station form evidence

This is an original 1995–2010 suburban station grammar, maintained at roughly
15 years of service. The current gate adds shared baked manufacture, causal
age and external-image delivery to the accepted form. No reference pixels
enter geometry or materials. It remains a lead-review checkpoint.

The dated Pennsylvania (2006), Louisiana (2008) and California (2009) photos
prove those operating configurations existed in the period. They do not establish
construction years or surveyed sizes. Colorado adds a western region; its capture
year is unverified and its tanker hides much of the forecourt. Authors, rights,
direct files and unavailable downloads are in [refs/SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md).

## Grammar and circulation

`BuildingSpec` is fuel-independent. `wall_with_openings`, `storefront`,
`glazed_leaf`, `rooftop_unit`, folded coping, scuppers and service assemblies
are reusable building parts. A strip mall can repeat tenant storefront bays,
share walls and use the same roof/services; it need not import a station-specific
store mesh. Fuel equipment, brand definition and corner site live separately.

Four grammar topologies support two, three or four islands; perpendicular,
parallel or 30-degree canopy placement; four global corner rotations; 56–68 ft
store widths and bounded depths; brick, smooth/split CMU or EIFS; two brands;
monument/pylon signs at the corner or south/west frontage; cooler on selected variants. The explicit proof seeds
remain fixed while other seeds vary dimensions, brand, sign and corner within
those envelopes. Unsupported controls and colliding island envelopes fail.

Every proof has entrances on its two adjacent streets, as requested. The corners
rotate rather than inventing a driveway through a nonstreet property line.
The pure geometry check sweeps a **5.70 × 2.14 m pickup body** through both
entrances and an outer fueling lane using forward-only straights and 4.8 m
center-radius arcs. It samples straights at no more than .12 m and turns at
one degree. The parked-store row is treated as occupied, and sign foundations are obstacles. Island rectangles
conservatively enclose their round ends. This proves the specified pickup route,
not tanker delivery, all simultaneous fueling movements, suspension motion,
traffic capacity or a civil-engineering design. `circulation.jpg` exposes the
paths rather than hiding behind a passing constant.

## Brands and trade dress review

upright piers, flat-ended and without an enclosing badge. **Latchline:** graphite
`#2C373D`, periwinkle `#8E97C7`, cool off-white; two offset squared brackets.
