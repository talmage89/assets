# Church construction checkpoint

Original town churches, maintained in 2010. The nominal construction dates,
names and parcel plans are fictional. This is a form checkpoint, not a claim
of surveyed replicas, structural design or code certification.

The historic church uses a six-bay nave, load-bearing brick, stepped
buttresses, recessed lancets, a rose, a corner tower and a later parish hall.
The mid-century church uses repeated laminated A bents, low brick sidewalls,
a glazed gable, a separate bell frame and a U-shaped education/garden group.
The buildings share a module interface but deliberately have different plans.

## Evidence and dimensions

HABS IL-1223, First Congregational Church, Western Springs, sheets 3, 5, 9,
11 and 14, supplies the measured-plan/section discipline, masonry reveals,
entry gauge and window construction. It is a 1920s Prairie/Gothic example,
not the stylistic template for the invented 1908 church. The Roanoke First
Baptist nomination (1898–1900) supports a six-bay brick nave, slate roof,
front bell tower and later one-story hall. PHMC distinguishes Gothic lancets,
steep roofs and buttresses from Romanesque round arches and heavier massing.

The 1961 Chapel for the Children nomination documents low brick walls,
laminated timber, a dominant triangular roof, gable stained glass, flat-roof
offices and a meditation garden. Washington DAHP's A-frame guide supports
the 1950s/60s suburban type. Neither source supplies a universal church size.
Chosen module dimensions are recorded explicitly rather than described as
measurements of a photograph.

ADA 1991 is the period reference for the invented 1996 retrofit; the Access
Board's current guide is a readable construction cross-check. Provide level
landings and rail extensions, edge protection and a route from both street
and accessible stalls. IBC assembly provisions motivate outward door swings,
panic bars and separated side/rear exits. No playable interior or occupancy
certificate is provided. Geometry checks must measure exported surfaces;
configured dimensions alone are not evidence of clearances.

## Review gate

Use clear low front-side sun, eye-height 35–50 mm heroes and native library
town context. Judge source and external-image exports against photographs.
Begin every critique with color/value and name the neutral used to balance
the reference. No docs gallery is committed at this checkpoint.
