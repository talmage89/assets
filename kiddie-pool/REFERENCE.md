# Dimensions and original design

The original **Minnow / Petal 60** is a rigid molded HDPE wading pool. Every
vertex and texel comes from our own analytic scripts. [SOURCES.md](refs/SOURCES.md)
credits the ignored inspection photographs and product measurements.

The [Home Hardware assembled dimensions](https://www.homehardware.ca/en/60-economy-kids-pool/p/7154072)
and [McCoy's listing](https://www.mccoys.com/shop/p/8288433-052303-30/gracious-living-1002-mayblusz-12-pool-60-in-dia-100-gal-round-polyethylene-blue)
identify a 60 in diameter, 12 in high molded polyethylene pool: **1524 ×
304.8 mm**. We use these assembled dimensions, not shipping size. The
[manufacturer's Econo Pool page](https://www.graciousliving.com/product-page/round-kiddie-pool)
confirms HDPE, rolled rim and nonslip floor construction; its current dimension
fields list smaller 36/42-inch variants, so it is not our 60-inch size source.
A [59 × 11.4 inch rigid pool listing](https://www.big5sportinggoods.com/store/details/funsicle-59-wading-pool/3305151410003)
provides a near-size cross-check.

The retailer photograph is a real molded shell with slight wall waviness and a
printed pattern. The manufacturer's smaller pool image may be a visualization;
it is used only for construction inspection. We copy neither printed waves,
raised dots, logo nor molded layout. Our original twelve wall flutes, petal
medallion, concentric traction rings and blue palette are authored independently.

Wall gauge 1.8 mm, floor 2.4 mm and curled-rim section are design assumptions;
references do not specify gauge. The section uses a real return underneath the
rim. The optional water surface is at **Z=140 mm above ground** (137.6 mm above
the inner floor). It is not the product's rated maximum fill. The checker
measures its actual height, inner-wall clearance and floor separation from the
meshes. Five original grass blades lie on the wet lip. Sun fade follows exposed
normals/upper rim; mineral residue follows a 145 mm waterline and interior
normal; dirt follows the floor-wall cavity and ground edge.

Used-object weather calibration uses the front round pool in Adam's Northwest
Estate Auctions lot 191 (February 2026), `estate-used.jpg`. Exact age and size
are unknown. Its ordinary outdoor discoloration is compared with the shipped
GLB in the same estimated camera framing; original floor patterns differ.
The KSL photo is an auxiliary used oblong pool, not a round silhouette match.
The forum's old image URL returned HTTP 403 and is not part of the build.
