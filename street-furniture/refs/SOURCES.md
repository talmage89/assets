Additional primary records, read online (not download dependencies):

- [Tolar 2007 shelter specification in LA County's procurement](https://file.lacounty.gov/SDSInter/bos/supdocs/45154.pdf): period 13 ft shelter vocabulary and fabrication. Original roof profile and end design here, rather than the Spanish roof in that order.
- [Tolar Signature product data](https://tolarmfg.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/TolarOneSheet2023-WEB-Signature.pdf): modular aluminum shelters, roof alternatives, advertising and lighting. Manufacturer copyright; contemporary documentation of the long-running family.
- [Heinz History Center, machine 54993](https://heinzhistorycenter.emuseum.com/objects/54993/machine-vending): measured historical coin news rack, 48.75 × 19.25 × 17.125 in H/W/D; printed steel coin compartment, access door, window. Institutional object measurement, not a manufacturer's tolerance drawing.
- [Kaspar Wire Works](https://kasparwireworks.com/project/usa-today): coin mechanism/fabrication history; no real masthead reused.
- [Smithsonian, POM APM made 1994](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_1851403): 56 cm head assembly, 18 cm width, 12/17.5 cm depth measures. Our 48 cm compact mechanical casting and 1.52 m installed height are declared authoring choices within this size class, rather than a claim of identical housing dimensions.
- [POM housing/mechanism product documentation](https://www.pom.com/mechanisms/apm/): zinc upper housing, separate iron vault and rotary or LCD states. Author's dimensions and service hinge remain estimates.
- [Victor Stanley traditional streetscape dimensions in Ballston's agency standard](https://www.ballstonva.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/2021-02-04_Ballston-BID-2020-Placemaking-Plan.pdf): 28-1/8 in diameter × 38-5/8 in receptacle, slats, plastic liner, surface mounting. Our original open-center lid reduces height to .960 m and body diameter to .677 m.
- [USPS original historian record](https://about.usps.com/who/profile/history/pdf/mail-collection-boxes.pdf): URL currently returns 404 to the fetcher; archived copy above preserves the cited historical dimensions.
