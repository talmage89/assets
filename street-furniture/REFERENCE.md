The sign family uses the existing Ground **.1524 m / 6 in** construction grid.
The bus route plate was raised 7.9 mm after the form checkpoint. Its actual
exported lower edge is now 2.1336 m, the cited seven-foot clearance target.
Furniture roots are the center of their ground footprint; `*_SidewalkSocket`
uses finished pavement grade, while `*_Anchor` sockets give actual anchor
positions. The shelter socket refers to its slab underside and its bench
sits on the slab's .150 m top. Buried footings and rods are omitted.

Blender +Z up, −Y front; glTF +Y up, +Z front. The placement example reuses
native Ground module dimensions/UVs. Its wider transit sidewalk is assembled
from three stock five-foot panels; it is a presentation example, not a claim
to have surveyed or accessibility-certified that street. Houses, cars and
Ground are presentation only and never included in either new export.
