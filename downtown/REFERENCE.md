HABS CT-349's 1977 site plan is used to compare the party-wall urban grain and
variable building depth. It is a plan reference; the small preview does not
support precise sub-foot dimensions. No unmeasured dimension is presented as a
survey measurement. The raw/fresh-import checks separately measure what we built.

Three pairs use HABS photographs, with camera fits and residuals disclosed.
Two are monochrome: only value and form can be judged, never hue. For the
color row, the pale sidewalk and white window trim are the white-balance
references. Lighting/color checks remain preliminary at this quick-material gate.
