HABS CT-349's 1977 site plan is used to compare the party-wall urban grain and
variable building depth. It is a plan reference; the small preview does not
support precise sub-foot dimensions. No unmeasured dimension is presented as a
survey measurement. The raw/fresh-import checks separately measure what we built.

The active comparisons use three HABS views for form, a native storefront
detail cropped from the same Foster fit, and two separate color
photographs: Auburn's sunny street and Louisiana's overcast painted brick,
hoods and pressed cornice. The stated photographic neutrals are Auburn's pale
unpainted apron and Louisiana's pale hood fascia. Neither is a gray card;
channel gains, exact pixels and estimated-camera residuals are recorded in
`build/cameras.json`. Cropped photographs can have unknown lens shift or
perspective correction. We compare original corresponding construction rather
than claiming to recover a surveyed facade. Rejected Galena and storefront-only
fits are documented in REVIEW.md and do not calibrate material color.

Regional cross-check: Ouray's National Register district nomination, section 7,
places mostly one- and two-story brick commercial buildings alongside occasional
large landmarks. That supports the western block's low skyline; its specific
Second Empire hotel and opera house are outside this grammar. Gonzales's National Register nomination, section 7 pages 6–7, corroborates
1–3-story party-wall rows occupying full lot depths, recessed entries, altered
display glass, ghost signs and more restrained later commercial fronts.
The gallery remains an authored interpretation of the NPS storefront/awning
construction guidance, not a surveyed copy of a Texan building.
