# Round 2 front plan

Preserve the accepted body, axle centers, bumpers, mechanisms and sockets.
Use the existing two-meter engine bay more convincingly: lower the nose about
65 mm, carry a shallow crowned top continuously from the windshield base,
and replace the separate fender rings and cowl slabs with one fiberglass mold.

The inherited supported Catmull–Clark cage is the starting construction.
Its transverse stations describe the crown, falling shoulder and swept rear
fender; wheelhouse cuts and a 6 mm laminate give it actual openings and gauge.
Inspect the nose, axle and rear shoulder in transverse sections and raking
three-quarter light. Keep a denser independent mold for the normal bake.

The grille sits behind a rounded opening with real inward returns and a dark
radiator bed. Two sealed paired-lamp cassettes sit in cut pockets in the same
molding. Their bezels follow the pocket perimeter. A backed cowl parting line
replaces the flat yellow corner plates. Crossview bracket feet must be seated
on the changed actual hood surface, rather than left at their old positions.

Compare construction and visual weight with the two service photographs
`service-front.jpg` (IC) and `vision-service.jpg` (Blue Bird), without copying
either maker's front. The original horizontal recessed grille and paired lamps
remain fictional. The old exported views and cameras are archived in
`renders/round2-before/`; subsequent comparisons keep at least 800 native
pixels on each side. Changes proceed in small patches with source previews
and working commits before UV/bake work.
