Online primary references, viewed:

- USGS, [Circular 1246, Lewis and Clark's Observations and Measurements of Geomorphology and Hydrology, and Changes with Time](https://pubs.usgs.gov/circ/2003/1246/report.pdf), 2003, J.A. Moody, R.H. Meade and D.R. Jones: riffle/pool and point-bar/cutbank relationships, pp36–37. The PDF endpoint was intermittently unavailable; no local copy claimed.
- USGS, [SIR 2006-5075](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/sir20065075), regional bankfull width/depth equations; use is a scale check, not an assertion that a regional curve specifies a normal-flow surface.
- FHWA, [HEC-23 guidance](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/hydraulics/pubs/hec/hec23.pdf), older edition DG8, used to cross-check pier/abutment riprap placement and its maintenance limits.
- Ohio DOT, [Waterway Permits Manual](https://www.transportation.ohio.gov/business/publications/waterway-permits-manual), stream restoration guidance: bankfull stage at floodplain overflow; riffle keyed into banks. This supplements the USGS channel descriptions, rather than supplying a prescribed normal-flow width.
- Florida DOT, [2010 standard drawing register](https://www.fdot.gov/roadway/DS/10/STDs.shtm), indexes 20500 bearing pads, 20900 approach slabs, 21100 strip seal joints, 21210 utility conduit. These support assembly vocabulary; only 20130's dimensions are reproduced numerically.
- Installed DejaVu Sans / Noto Sans under their open licenses for fictional county/date lettering. No real brand, municipal seal or model identity is reproduced.

Local pair products remain ignored. Cropping, assumed-neutral balancing and
estimated cameras are logged with the comparisons; original photos stay intact.
