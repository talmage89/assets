# Review log / Orbit 14

## Pass 1 / source and photographic cloth comparison

Inspected the actual Nayand in-service photograph, all five views in `renders/eevee-review/`, the three revised views in `renders/eevee-review-2/`, and `renders/source-review/match_used-pair.jpg`.

Problems found: an alpha-tested fine net disappeared at hero distance; the first bed weave exposed too much of the legs; the first mesh spent over 11k triangles on tiny round bindings and fasteners; closed annular/U-shaped UV islands wasted atlas area; the initially fully covered springs did not read at the pad edge. The source photo comparison also exposed that the padding remained too regular relative to real creased cloth.

Corrections: optional BLEND coverage in the shared kit, independently tested by actual export/import while retaining the legacy MASK fixture; denser original net weave and 93.75% delivered bed coverage; cloth ribbons for bindings and laces; source-only bolt/swage detail; real separate tube sections and sewn panels for efficient UV islands. Added a slight front inner-lip roll to expose the installed hooks/coils and kept high-source seam puckering. Both sides of the pad use the same physically exposed-normal mask: blue top visibly faded, underside dark. Foam fading follows height; zinc corrosion follows sockets/weld stations; analytic spring rust is restricted to the hook zones and is nonmetallic.

Current game mesh is 7,922 triangles. Net and mat remain alpha cards, and all 84 springs use crossed original analytic cards. A few straight points on each W brace establish exact ground contact. The sliding zipper has an actual channel, and the three auxiliary buckle pairs are displayed released so its travel stays clear. This is an unloaded cloth asset, not a cloth simulation.

Photographic matching assumes the reference is the same diameter class; no EXIF or measured frame size is available. The estimated lens reaches the solver's 180 mm bound, with about 10 px landmark RMS. That is a useful same-framing cloth comparison, not a claim about the original camera or an exact product copy. The photographed pole/leg arrangement and red cover differ from this original blue six-leg design.

## Pass 2 / exported asset

Inspected the first exported-GLB hero, fitting, fabric and spring close-ups and the full eleven-view checkpoint contact sheet. This rejected delivery had black/missing patches on the padding. Sampling its 4K atlas localized misses to nonplanar cloth panels and narrow sewn/laced ribbons. The source renderer and finalizer had chosen different diagonals. Both high and game now explicitly triangulate the same cloth quads with BEAUTY before saving; a new bake guard rejects any missed/black interior BaseColor samples. The corrected bake measured zero in both categories.

An independent physical check also caught a 0.27 mm ground gap from the arbitrary clocking of generic parallel-transport tube sections. The W braces now use their known bending plane to clock a bottom vertex onto grade, and measure 0 m. Retained spring-alpha pixel centers reach the mat webbing and the frame; this is separate from GLB serialization. The spring cards now carry analytic cylindrical normals and 28 turns, with rough nonmetallic hook oxide. `WEAR` was verified to darken the clean net and remove hook oxide without changing weave coverage.

Final review: opened all eleven rebuilt Cycles images in `renders/final/`, the Eevee hero, their full contact sheet, the final documentation montage, and `renders/delivery-review-glb/match_used-pair.jpg`. The padding no longer has the rejected projection patches. Its top is visibly lighter than the protected underside; the net hangs between supported hems, and exposed coils and rusty hooks read beneath the rolled lip. The final photographic pair retains the same framing limitations described above. The blue pad is still smoother and more regularly segmented than the creased red reference, and the bed lets the leg silhouettes show more clearly against the white studio floor. Those are remaining realism weaknesses, not evidence that the photographed trampoline has the same weave.

`make` and a separate `make check` passed after the fixes. Delivery: 7,922 triangles, two materials, three meshes, three shared 1024² maps and 1,812,712 GLB bytes. Unique UV overlap/outside/degenerate counts are all zero. Normal unit error is 1.19e-7. The BaseColor bake sampled 3,562,065 interior texels with zero missed or black samples. Both 33-position zipper sweeps found zero penetration. Independent physical checks measure 0 m ground gap, 4.2672002 m frame diameter, and 1.63 / 3.03 mm retained-alpha overlap at the sampled spring's inner/outer attachments. This last test samples one radial spring, not every hook or continuous solid contact.

Remaining delivery limits: crossed spring cards flatten at extreme close/grazing angles. Fine alpha weave can shimmer or show transparency sorting differences between engines; configure the normal transparent pass and temporal antialiasing. Padding wrinkles and weathering have less fine variation than the source after fitting the shared 1024 atlas. The zipper slider moves independently, but the net has no animated opening or cloth deformation. No ground, grass wear or runtime trampoline simulation ships. The three final docs JPGs are each below 200 KB and at most 1600 pixels wide.

## Phase 2 / finishing wear, exported comparisons

Compared the exported `renders/wear-review-glb/match_used-pair.jpg` with Nayand’s in-service photograph. The first revision made the exposed top lighter and dirtied the feet, but the entry scuffs read as pale clouds and handling grime remained too weak. Rejected that entry treatment. Inspected all three source checkpoints in `renders/wear-check/`, including the protected underside and ground deposits.

Refined the contact mask into broken shoe/knee-sized marks beside the zip, with grime held at the nearby stitched seam. Reduced the pale scuff strength, added shallow compression pleats in the normal source, and retained much darker sides/underside. Accepted the second exported pair, `renders/wear-review-2-glb/match_used-pair.jpg`, and the exported hero/entry checkpoints in `renders/wear-check-2-glb/`. Fade is now apparent at hero distance; the smaller entry marks are clearest in the close-up. Ground dirt belongs to the lower 200 mm of the steel, rather than the entire frame. Geometry, UV policy, alpha tiles, pivots and texture sizes are unchanged.

The reference’s red cover, sunlit lawn and more heavily creased padding remain different from this original blue studio asset. The calibrated pair supports weather/cloth critique, not an exact color or product match; its existing estimated 180 mm fit and 10.2 px RMS are unchanged. Cloth silhouette creases and transparent-weave limitations are outside this finishing pass.

Final evidence for this pass: inspected all eleven 1440 px Cycles finals, the Eevee hero, the complete `renders/final/contact.jpg`, and the final turnaround/details documentation. All final view and accepted pair metadata identifies the same GLB SHA-256. `make -C trampoline` passed; the atlas still contains 7,922 triangles, two materials, three meshes and three 1024² images, now 1,871,324 GLB bytes. Every baked channel reports zero missed interior samples; BaseColor also reports zero black samples. The raw pre/post GLB comparison in `../build/geometry-preservation.json` measures identical triangle-position multisets (to 1 μm) and exact unchanged slider transforms/extras. This proves preservation separately from the sampled support and clearance checks. No kit code changed.
