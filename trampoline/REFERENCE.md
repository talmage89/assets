# Orbit 14 / measured original design

The assembly is original and unbranded. It combines common construction dimensions, not a copy of any manufacturer's complete design.

| Adopted dimension/construction | Evidence and distinction |
| --- | --- |
| 14 ft / 4.2672 m outer frame | [Skywalker 12/14 ft product specifications](https://www.skywalkertrampolines.com/products/12-14-round-trampoline) identify the nominal 14 ft class. |
| Frame height 890 mm, 38 mm tube, approximately 2.69 m total height | [JumpPRO 14 ft specifications](https://www.trampolines.co.uk/products/14ft-jumppro-black-round-trampoline-with-enclosure-j14b): these dimensions; woven polypropylene bed. This asset's frame construction is galvanized rather than their black finish. |
| Twelve frame sections, six W braces, twelve leg extensions, six enclosure poles and 84 springs | Skywalker manufacturer manual, SWTC1400 part list and assembly, mirrored [PDF](https://www.bedbathandbeyond.com/downloads/pdf/SWRT14%20Manual%20-%20English%20%26%20Spanish.pdf). The PDF contains other revisions/models too; the cited SWTC1400 list is the 84-spring one. |
| Mat diameter 3.81 m, pole tube OD 25.4 mm, foam OD 52 mm | Original fitted design dimensions consistent with the 14 ft class and photographic proportions, not manufacturer-certified measurements. |
| Loaded spring/ring span 190 mm; coil OD 16 mm | Original assembly choices. These are stretched installed geometry, not a quoted unloaded spring length. Ends connect the mat's sewn ring to the frame. |
| Net base/top radii 1.885 / 1.919 m, up to 55 mm top sag, 26 mm radial bagging | Original used-cloth shape, calibrated visually against the in-service photograph. Age is not known; the photo is not presented as a catalog image or exact 14 ft measurement. |
| Net pitch/thread 8 / 1.25 mm; bed pitch/thread 4 / 3.2 mm | Original render-scale weave approximations. No measured manufacturer weave specification is claimed. |

[Nayand's in-service enclosure photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trampoline_with_enclosure.jpg) shows red sewn padding with creases, a black woven mat, hanging mesh, perimeter binding and zip entry. Our palette is faded blue, poles carry foam, and construction is six-leg rather than the photographed arrangement. Matching uses photographic framing to critique cloth response, not trace or copy geometry. The camera fit explicitly assumes this photograph is the same diameter class.

All vertices and map pixels come from scripts. The mat, net and repeated spring impostors deliberately reuse reserved atlas regions. No grass or ground wear belongs to the exported asset.
