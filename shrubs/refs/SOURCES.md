## Botanical sources (read online; no executable content downloaded)

- [NC State: Green Velvet](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/buxus-green-velvet/):
  rounded hybrid, 2–3 ft tall/wide; opposite, entire, deep green blades;
  mature brown/new green stems; shade reduces foliage. These are cultivar ranges,
  not a survey of the photographed plant.
- [Clemson: Boxwood](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/boxwood/): Green Velvet
  around 3 ft tall × 4 ft wide (0.91 × 1.22 m). Difference from NC State is retained,
  not averaged into a false precision.
- [NYBG: Types of Boxwood](https://libguides.nybg.org/c.php?g=983941&p=7126817):
  Korean parent leaves 1/2–3/4 in (12.7–19.1 mm); littleleaf 1/3–1 in
  (8.5–25.4 mm); common parent glossy convex blades, paler yellow-green backs.
  The brief's 15–25 mm hybrid blade range is an authored choice within parental
  ranges, not a published Green Velvet-specific blade measurement.
- [Virginia Tech: Selecting Landscape Plants: Boxwoods](https://www.pubs.ext.vt.edu/426/426-603/426-603.html):
  Alex X. Niemiera; all photos by author. Foundation use, dense clipped shell,
  3–6 in/year growth; thinning needed to admit light to the interior.
- [NC State: Hydrangea macrophylla](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hydrangea-macrophylla/):
  3–6 ft tall/wide; opposite serrated coarse leaves; summer mopheads, soil-dependent
  blue/pink coloration. Source distinguishes individual floret size from heads.
- [Virginia Tech Dendrology: Bigleaf hydrangea](https://dendro.cnre.vt.edu/dendrology/syllabus/factsheet.cfm?ID=285):
  opposite 4–8 in serrated leaves, paler underside with raised veins; head clusters
  8 in across (20.3 cm), summer bloom.
- [Morton Arboretum: Big-leaved hydrangea](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/big-leaved-hydrangea/):
  rounded multi-stem 3–5 ft; 'All Summer Beauty' mopheads to 8 in. The brief's
  10–15 cm leaves and 15–20 cm heads select the compact part of these ranges.
