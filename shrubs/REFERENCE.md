The boxwood is maintained but several weeks past shearing. Small uneven lobes
are deliberately much shallower than the maple's scaffold masses. The front
faces −Y; +Y models a more shaded house-facing side with fewer low leaves and
localized old brown-green blades. Basal branches remain visible below the
foliage. Soil is a small root-entry patch rather than an exported garden bed.

Jim Robbins' Green Velvet near porch columns is the primary hue/material and
regrowth reference. Niemiera's clipped rounded specimen is the second form
reference; its cultivar is not treated as Green Velvet. His house-foundation
photo establishes the much darker shrub values relative to a lawn. These are
ordinary planted settings with summer/leaf-on appearance, not controlled
color charts. Different specimens, unknown lens, pruning history and lighting
limit quantitative matching. Pixel fits are comparisons, not surveys.

Hydrangea evidence is researched now; no hydrangea geometry is built until the
boxwood checkpoint has been reviewed.
