Additional primary sources consulted online:

- [HUD homeowner resources](https://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/manufactured-home-resources): June 15, 1976 construction-code boundary. HUD code compliance is not asserted for these game meshes.
- [HUD National Commission report](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/portal/sites/default/files/pdf/National-Commission-Manufactured-Housing-final-report.pdf): pre-federal ANSI A119.1, expanded body/frame requirements by 1969; trade-association/state adoption varied.
- [Minnesota historical standards](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/1350.0100/): distinguishes pre-June 15, 1976 ANSI A119.1/NFPA 501B from the federal code. Primary state rule, not generalized nationwide equivalence.
- [HUD Housing in the Seventies](https://www.huduser.gov/publications/pdf/hud-968.pdf): twelve-foot sections in mass production from 1962; historical size comparator, not a manufacturing drawing.
- [HUD Home Builder Guide for Manufactured Housing](https://www.huduser.gov/Publications/PDF/buildergd.pdf), 2000, HUD/PATH: siting, community integration and surveyed lot standards. The authored parks include common-area allowances, so gross density differs from net lot area.
- [Johnson City municipal mobile-home code](https://ecode360.com/12624699): minimum 40 ft space frontage and 20 ft internal drives. Used as a compact established-park comparator; no claim of historical local approval.
- [Forsyth County official UDC, §11-8.1](https://www.forsythco.com/Departments-Offices/Planning-Community-Development/Unified-Development-Code): 24 ft two-way private roads / no parking, 40 × 75 ft minimum spaces. Newer park uses a 24 ft loop and larger lots. Code dimensions are a design comparator, not a permit-compliance assertion.
- [Polk County Mobile Home Park Ordinance](https://cms4files1.revize.com/polkcounty/departments/planning_and_zoning/docs/planning/mhpark.bocapp.5.3.2021.pdf), §303: 20 ft interior streets, 10 ft drives, two off-street spaces. Modern dimensional comparison only.
- [Baldwin, 1970, Mobile Home Parks—Design, Construction and Costs](https://scholarsmine.mst.edu/islchp/15/): bibliographic research; PDF retrieval denied (403), **not used for numerical claims**.

The nominal generated widths (12/14/16 and 24/28/32 ft) and lengths
(52–80 ft) are original grammar choices within the brief's ranges. The
manufacturer sheets establish the scale and the distinction between actual
and nominal sizes; they do not prove a specific 1972 or 1996 model. Roof
profiles, window rhythm, chassis gauge and colors are authored construction
inferences from photographs and the cited standards.

All reused meshes, maps and credits remain native to houses, Ground, fences,
shed, utility, mailbox, trash-cart, patio-set, gas-grill, playground, trees,
shrubs and cars. Local shed/pole/playground source adapters use quick
materials without modifying those families. Open-license DejaVu Sans
