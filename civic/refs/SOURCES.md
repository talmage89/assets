Additional primary sources, viewed online:

- [HABS MA-995 measured town-hall drawings](https://www.loc.gov/resource/hhh.ma0298.sheet?st=gallery): Lowell town hall, 1830, altered 1896; facade organization and masonry opening returns, not a 1930 dimensional template.
- [HABS LA-1127 first floor plan](https://www.loc.gov/resource/hhh.la0072.sheet/?sp=2&st=image): apparatus/living separation; sheet 2.
- [HABS NJ-357 post-office measured sheets](https://www.loc.gov/resource/hhh.nj0699.sheet?st=gallery): opening/construction comparison for the small post-office variant.
- [Fire Engineering, From the Ground Up, 1989](https://www.fireengineering.com/firefighting/from-the-ground-up-constructing-or-reconstructing-a-modern-firehouse-2/): roughly 15ft-wide bays, single 40ft/double 80ft depth, 12–14ft doors, and 50ft rear maneuvering. New station uses wider 18ft centers and 60ft depth, an authored allowance.
- [Tubac Fire District design guide](https://www.tubacfiredistrict.org/files/8d0d2dc8f/TFD-Fire-Station-Design-Guide.pdf): 14×14ft bay doors as a dimensional cross-check, later than the station's construction.
- [WBDG fire-station guide](https://legacy.wbdg.org/building-types/community-services/fire-station): functional adjacency and vehicle exhaust removal.
- [ADA Chapter 4](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch04/): 36in route, 1:20 walking-surface limit, 1:12 ramp limit, 60in turning landing, handrail/edge protection. Used as a later accessible-entry reference, not a compliance certificate.

All town names, inscriptions, plaques and agency markings are fictional. Installed DejaVu fonts are original graphics inputs.
