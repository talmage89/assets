# Parking garage construction basis

This is an original form checkpoint, not a surveyed reproduction or a structural,
fire, traffic or accessibility certification. Published dimensions and authored
choices are kept separate. The dated references are registered, with authors and
rights, in [SOURCES](refs/SOURCES.md). Photographs never enter asset materials.

| Feature | Published basis | Authored construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stall and aisle | Kimley-Horn/CCDC, parking layout table, p25: 8 ft 6 in × 18 ft with a 24 ft aisle gives a 60 ft module | 2.5908 × 5.4864m stalls, 7.3152m aisle, 18.288m double-loaded span |
| Precast grid | PCI MNL-129 (1997) ch2: long spans remove aisle columns; PCI regional FAQ: 12ft tees suit 36/48ft bays | 3.6576m tee pitch, 10.9728m structural grid; partial terminal bays retain their own supports |
| Tee section | PCI construction vocabulary and the 2014 Z22 photograph: flange, tapered twin stems, bearing pads, girders and corbels | 24in / .6096m overall tee, 4in / .1016m flange, 50mm topping; 120–190mm tapered webs; 20mm longitudinal joint |
| Floor spacing | PCI ch2 discusses passenger clearance and the effect of beams, tee stems and signs; CCDC p16 discusses approximately 10ft levels | 11ft / 3.3528m, selected to retain van entry headroom under the source beam section |
| Parking ramp | PCI §2.4, p2-9: parking grades generally ≤6%; 2009 IBC §406.2.5: 1:15 maximum for ramps used for parking | 11ft rise over 192ft: 5.7292%, a whole 16-tee run |
| Speed ramp | PCI p2-9: about 12% limit; CCDC p14: 12–14%, 10ft half-gradient transitions at both ends for large grade breaks | 11ft rise, 120ft total run, including two 10ft transitions; 10.0% middle, 5.0% transitions; no stalls on this ramp |

| Clear height | 2009 IBC §§406.2.2 and 406.3.4: 7ft; ADA §502.5: 98in through van stall, aisle and complete vehicle route | Entry bar lower edge 2.4892m; source soffits are independently ray-measured after export |

The new precast columns, 30in / .762m square with chamfered corners, are an
authored conservative section, not a copied manufacturer's product. Corbels have
sloping undersides, separate elastomeric seats and girder end bearings. The
cast-in-place variant has 220mm slabs and transverse .61m-wide downstand PT
beams, .76m overall below the deck, on monolithic columns. Its anchor pocket
patches are modeled at exposed slab ends. No tendon/rebar design is supplied.

The long garage has one flat parking bay and one full-rise sloping parking bay.
West landings meet the low end; east landings meet the preceding ramp's high
end. The highest drive route returns along the flat aisle and terminates there;
it does not drive onto the missing next ramp. Internal barriers separate the
different elevations. End landings use the same tee construction overhead.

The smaller garage has one flat double-loaded bay and a separate 24ft speed
ramp along its rear. Its clear end landings are deliberately generous; the
result is less area-efficient than a large municipal structure. These are
different plan masses and different parking programs, not two recolored seeds.

The three principal photographic comparisons are the Z22 interior (2014),
Pleasant Hill exterior (2007) and Harmon exterior (2005 structure, photographed
2012). The latter compares a cropped stack of flat-deck openings rather than
pretending that this original three-level garage is the photographed larger
building. EXIF's approximate 33mm equivalent constrains the interior fit.
Reference-neutral patches, gains, landmarks, crop, estimated lens, residuals
and independent Blender projection checks are recorded in build/matches/.

The Chicago screened wall and Alewife roof/atrium photographs are secondary
construction/occupancy references. They are not asserted as geometry matches:
the Alewife atrium is a different architectural type and the roof has a canopy
that is not part of these original open-top designs.
