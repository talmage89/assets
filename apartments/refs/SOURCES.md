Additional primary sources consulted online:

- [HUD MPS resources](https://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/minimum-property-standards-resources): 1994 Handbook 4910.1 and 1997 technical suitability context.
- [1974 Federal Register](https://archives.federalregister.gov/issue_slice/1974/7/24/26893-26903.pdf): incorporation of 1973 MPS, Volume 2 Multifamily Housing.
- [Access Board handrail dimensions](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch05/): 34–38 inch grasping height and circular 1.25–2 inch section.
- [Tennessee Fire Marshal plan review](https://comptroller.aem.tn.extglb.tn.gov/commerce/fire/codes-enforcement/fire-plans-review/general.html): 42 inch guards, 4 inch sphere comparator. Contemporary guidance used conservatively for maintained replacement rails, not attributed to every historical jurisdiction.

The DAHP photographic crops are generated locally by src/refs.py, with page/crop metadata.
Photographs establish form, not surveyed dimensions. Neutral balancing is disclosed in REVIEW.md.
