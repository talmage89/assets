Text sources (read online, not downloaded):

- UF/IFAS, Gilman et al., [ENH-200: Acer rubrum](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/ST041).
  Irregular, moderate-density oval/round crown; upward-growing branches and attachment
  problems; smooth young gray bark, furrowed older bark; surface roots; leaf description.
- Patrick Breen, Oregon State University, [Acer rubrum](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/acer-rubrum).
  Ascending branching; opposite, usually three but sometimes five lobed leaves,
  5–10(13) cm; serrated margins, medium/dark green above, silver below; surface roots.
- Mark H. Brand, University of Connecticut, [Acer rubrum](https://plantdatabase.uconn.edu/detail.php?pid=13).
  Young elliptical/pyramidal form spreading toward rounded/oval; 2–4 in leaves,
  3/5 lobes; ash/silver-gray young bark, scaly gray-brown old bark.
- Jodi Sulpizio, Penn State Extension, [Rain Garden Plants: Red Maple](https://extension.psu.edu/rain-garden-plants-red-maple).
  Summer habit photograph used above and landscape context.

Neither photo supplies a survey or tree age. Their comparable specimen scale is
estimated from the visible street/bench context, not claimed as measured height,
DBH or a proven 20-year age. Absolute dimensions come from the brief; proportions
are compared against both photographs. Camera fits are approximate biological
landmarks across different individuals, not a surveyed reconstruction.
