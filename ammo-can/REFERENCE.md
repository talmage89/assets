# M2A1-style .50 caliber ammo can

The military specification is **MIL-DTL-3060G**, assembly drawing **7553296**. The government text classifies M2A1 as a .32 cubic-foot airtight container and requires free cover removal/operation, securely retained gasket, and freely mating hasp/latch. DLA's public catalog lists the revision history; its document links were expired during research. The government-authored Amendment 3 text was read through its public transcription; no login, messages or drawing requests were used. The separately controlled drawing is not reproduced in the specification. This visual asset does not claim load, seal or military certification.

The manufacturer/distributor product sheet at [Enigma Technical Solutions](https://enigmatechnicalsolutions.com/files/Ammunition/Small%20Arms%20Ammunition/M2A1%20METAL%20AMMUNITION%20BOX.pdf), corroborated by [Yugoimport](https://www.yugoimport.com/sites/default/files/documents/2023-12/M2A1%20METAL%20AMMUNITION%20BOX.pdf), explicitly states **305.6 mm length × 154.8 mm width × 190.5 mm height**, with minimum casing/lid gauges **0.8 / 1.2 mm**. These dimensions control this original model. A second manufacturer's drawings say 303 × 155 × 191 mm; that is a nearby production envelope, not a reason to mix dimensions. Manufacturer photos show a folded top handle, broad lid skirt/retainer, cam plate and lower wire bail, body-mounted hinge pin and pressed upper side reinforcement.

Authored estimates: 280 × 139.5 mm interior mouth class, body walls 0.8 mm, lid 1.2 mm, 4 mm corner radius, 3 mm hinge pin, 3 mm carrying wire, 1.5 mm latch sheet, and retained rubber gasket at the rim. Hardware dimensions and opening travel are measured and checked on the generated surfaces; they are not claimed drawing tolerances. Body +Z up, latch end front −Y; the long dimension is Y. Origin is the ground-footprint center.

Markings are a fictional storage lot, using an installed open-license DejaVu font with original stencil bridges. No real contractor, arsenal, logo or serial number is reproduced.
