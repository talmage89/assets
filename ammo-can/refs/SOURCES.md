Later validation/amendment entries also cover M2A2; this asset follows the requested older M2A1 type, not its added stacking ledge.

The government-authored MIL-DTL-3060G Amendment 3 (23 November 2015), publicly transcribed at https://govtribe.com/file/government-file/b01-attachment-0002-mil-dtl-3060g-ar-dot-pdf, was consulted for assembly drawing 7553296, free cover/hasp/latch operation and gasket retention/compression. The DLA catalog remains the authority for revision history. Its expiring document-image links returned 404 during this session. No login or drawing request was used. A manufacturer supplies the explicit numeric envelope; the specification points to the separately controlled assembly drawing rather than reproducing its dimensions.

The files named `interior.jpg` and `gasket.jpg` turned out to be manufacturer side/front dimension drawings (303 × 155 × 191 mm), rather than interior photos. Used only to inspect wall/hasp placement and compare tolerances; the explicit Enigma/Yugoimport 305.6 × 154.8 × 190.5 mm sheet controls the authored envelope. `open.jpg` is the front latch photograph; `hinge.jpg` is the actual opened-lid photograph. These filenames are retained so reference retrieval remains stable.
